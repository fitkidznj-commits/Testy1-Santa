## Creative angle

Lead with the one thing no other restaurant can claim: **you order dinner standing
waist-deep in the ocean.** The hook is the experience, not the food. Keep it fast,
sunny, real, and a little funny (wallet-over-the-head moment).

**Tone:** Keys-casual, warm, energetic. Sun, turquoise water, boat-party hum.
**Music:** Upbeat tropical/island groove, sound-on. Cut on the beat.
