# Islamorada Shrimp Shack — Video Scripts & Reel Concepts

Campaign: **"New Owners. Same Keys Soul."** · Companion to [`proposal.md`](proposal.md).
Video delivered as scripts/storyboards; a.i. STaRR renders finished cuts via HeyGen /
Creatomate as a production add-on (see
[brand guide stack](../_shared/brand-guide.md#5-standard-ai-automation-stack)).

> **Emotional throughline for every video:** take the viewer from *"it closed"* → *"wait,
> it's still here"* → *"and it's still the real thing."* The soul survived. Treat all
> ownership/date specifics as **"client to confirm."**

---

## Video Strategy

- **One hero film wins the community over** — the 60-second "Same Keys Soul" story. It's
  the pinned post, the website header, and the centerpiece of the relaunch.
- **A tight 30-second cut drives the reopening** — a punchy, appetite-forward version for
  ads, stories, and the countdown.
- **Reels carry daily reassurance + craving** — icons looking exactly as remembered, Hook
  & Cook in action, the screen door opening.
- **AI acceleration:** HeyGen produces a warm "letter from the new owners" presenter
  segment without a formal shoot; Creatomate turns a shared shot library + captions into
  branded reels on autopilot.

**Purpose:** Drive reopening-day traffic. Fast, warm, appetite-forward.

## 60-Second Video Script — "Same Keys Soul" (hero brand film)

**Purpose:** Win the community's trust. Emotional, cinematic, the pinned launch asset.
**Format:** 9:16 (with 16:9 variant for web) · cinematic underscore + ambient · sparse,
heartfelt on-screen text · optional HeyGen new-owner VO.

| Scene | Time | Shot (visual) | Voiceover (VO) | On-screen text | Audio |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–6s | Quiet exterior at dawn, "closed" stillness, faded sign | "For almost fifteen years, this little Shack wasn't just a place to eat…" | — | Ambient highway, soft piano in |
| 2 | 6–14s | Archival-style warm shots: full baskets, packed tables, laughter (or recreated) | "…it was a place to belong. Local shrimp. A basket worth the drive. A seat that felt like home." | "1 little Shack. 15 years." | Piano builds gently |
| 3 | 14–22s | The screen door closing; an empty chair; a beat of silence | "This June, the family who built it served their last plate. And the whole highway felt it." | "In June, it closed." | Music dips, single held note |
| 4 | 22–30s | A hand turns a key; light returns; the door opens again | "But the Shack was never just walls and a fryer. It was a feeling. And feelings like that don't close…" | "But the soul never left." | Warmth returns, strings rise |
| 5 | 30–40s | New family walking in, looking around with respect, getting to work | "…they get handed down. We're the new family behind the counter. And we made one promise: don't lose the soul." | "New owners." | Hopeful build |
| 6 | 40–50s | The icons return on screen, glorious: piña colada basket, grits, po'boy, Hook & Cook catch | "Same shrimp. Same Shack. Same screen door. Same Keys soul." | "Same. Keys. Soul. 🦐" | Music swells to peak |
| 7 | 51–57s | Regulars and new faces filling tables, golden hour, genuine joy | "We're not here to change what you love. We're here to keep it going." | "Welcome home." | Emotional peak holds |
| 8 | 57–60s | Logo over the open screen door at sunset; date stamp | "New owners. Same Keys soul. Pull up a chair — your seat's still here." | "Reopening [DATE] · Islamorada Shrimp Shack" | Resolve, warm button |

*Production options: a single half-day shoot (golden hour + screen door + dish hero shots
+ new-family footage) yields the 60s hero, the 30s cut, and 5+ reels. HeyGen can voice the
new-owner lines warmly without an on-camera shoot; the prior owners' blessing — if cleared
— could open the film for maximum trust.*

## Distribution Notes

- **Hero (60s):** Pin to IG + FB, embed on website header, use as the Day-14 launch beat
  (see [calendar](30-day-content-plan.md#30-day-calendar)).
- **30s cut:** Reopening-week ads + stories + countdown.
- **Reels:** Spread across the 30-day plan to carry daily reassurance and craving; Reel #7
  feeds the "Share Your Shack Memory" community loop.
- **AI render path:** shared shot library + captions → Creatomate templated reels; HeyGen
  for the new-owner presenter segment — keeping output daily without a daily shoot.
