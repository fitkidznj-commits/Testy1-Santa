# Project 4 — Islamorada Shrimp Shack Marketing Campaign

**Client:** Islamorada Shrimp Shack — 81901 Overseas Hwy, Islamorada, FL 33036
**Prepared by:** a.i. STaRR
**Campaign theme:** **"New Owners. Same Keys Soul."**
**Goal:** Create excitement around a new chapter for a beloved Islamorada institution —
while protecting the community trust and tradition that made it a local landmark.

---

## Executive Summary

The Islamorada Shrimp Shack is a Florida Keys institution. A family-run seafood spot on
the Overseas Highway, it spent roughly **15 years** building a fiercely loyal following
on fresh local Key West pink shrimp, a famous **piña-colada-batter shrimp basket**, a
guest-favorite **"Hook & Cook"** program for anglers, and the kind of laid-back,
real-Keys atmosphere visitors and locals both protect. It earned a national spotlight on
**Food Network's *Diners, Drive-Ins & Dives*** and holds a strong ~4.4-star reputation
across well over a thousand reviews. *(Ownership-transition details below are flagged
"client to confirm.")*

The Shack reportedly served its last day under its longtime owners in **June 2026**. That
ending is also the opportunity: a new owner, **David Epstein** *(client to confirm)*, can
reopen the doors with energy and a fresh menu — but the moment is delicate. The community
doesn't want a "new restaurant." They want **their** Shrimp Shack back, with the soul
intact.

a.i. STaRR proposes a launch campaign built on a single promise: **New owners. Same Keys
soul.** We reassure the regulars first, then build genuine excitement — a transition
announcement that thanks the past, a 30-day content system that re-introduces the
signature dishes, video that proves the soul survived, and a press release that frames the
reopening as continuity, not replacement.

**Headline outcome:** A reopening that the community *celebrates* instead of mourns — a
complete 30-day content system live in 30 days, daily posting, a review engine, and AI
automation that keeps the Shack's story in front of the Keys every single day.

**Recommended package:** Growth ($3,000/mo) — a single beloved location with a high-stakes
relaunch. See [`proposal.md`](proposal.md#11-pricing-recommendations).
