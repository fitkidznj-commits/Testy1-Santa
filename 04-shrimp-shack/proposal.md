## 1. Executive Summary

See [`README.md`](README.md#executive-summary) for the one-page version. In short: the
Islamorada Shrimp Shack is a ~15-year Keys institution with a national *Diners, Drive-Ins
& Dives* spotlight, a famous piña-colada-batter shrimp basket, a beloved "Hook & Cook"
program, and a ~4.4-star reputation built on fresh local shrimp and laid-back island soul.
After reportedly closing under its longtime owners in June 2026, the Shack is poised to
reopen under new ownership *(David Epstein — client to confirm)*.

The risk: a reopening that feels like a *replacement* alienates the regulars who made the
Shack a landmark. The opportunity: a relaunch that the community *welcomes home*. a.i.
STaRR will run a launch campaign anchored on one promise — **New Owners. Same Keys Soul.**
— reassuring locals first, then building genuine excitement, backed by a 30-day content
system, video that proves the soul survived, a community-first press release, and AI
automation that keeps it all running.

## 2. Research Findings

### The business *(client to confirm specifics)*
- **Name / location:** Islamorada Shrimp Shack, 81901 Overseas Hwy, Islamorada, FL 33036 —
  single location on the Overseas Highway.
- **History:** Family-owned and operated; the seafood concept ran for roughly **15 years**
  (the "Shrimp Shack" name dates to around **December 2011**, under owners with 20+ years
  in local restaurants). Run in recent years as a **mother–daughter team, Chef-Owner Jill
  Jahn and Morgan**. Reportedly served its **last day under longtime ownership on or
  around June 6, 2026.**
- **Signature menu:** Fresh **local Key West pink shrimp** is the identity. Fan favorites

  po'boy**, **shrimp scampi**, **cracked conch**, fritters, seafood pasta, tacos, salads,
  sandwiches, and burgers. Full bar.
- **"Hook & Cook":** A guest-favorite program — anglers bring their own daily catch and

- **Reputation:** ~**4.4 stars across well over 1,000 reviews** (Yelp shows 542+ reviews /
  476+ photos; aggregate following near ~1,800). Reviewers consistently praise freshness,
  value, and the relaxed, authentic-Keys vibe.
- **Hours (pre-closure, client to confirm post-reopen):** ~11:30am–9:00pm most days;
  closed Wednesdays.

### Customer segments
1. **Loyal locals & regulars** (most important here) — emotionally invested, protective,
   the ones who decide whether the relaunch lives or dies by word of mouth.
2. **Returning visitors** — travelers who made the Shack a tradition, searching to see "is

3. **New Keys visitors** — high-intent, review-driven, discovering via Google/Instagram.
4. **The fishing community** — anglers who built "Hook & Cook" into their routine.

### Competitor analysis (Islamorada / Upper Keys casual seafood)

| Competitor | Strength | Gap a.i. STaRR exploits for the Shrimp Shack |
|---|---|---|
| **Mrs. Mac's Kitchen (Key Largo)** | Iconic local institution; *survived an ownership change in 2023 by prioritizing locals* | A proven playbook — the Shack can run the same "we kept the soul" trust move, openly |
| **Islamorada Fish Company** | Brand recognition, waterfront, retail | The Shack wins on intimacy, "Hook & Cook," and pink-shrimp specialization |
| **Lazy Days / Lorelei** | Waterfront views, sunset brand | The Shack isn't selling the view — it's selling the *best shrimp on the highway* and the realest vibe |
| **Generic roadside seafood** | Convenience | The Shack has a *name*, a *story*, and a national TV credit — lean into legacy |

**Insight:** The Shack's competitive moat isn't a view or a buffet — it's **legacy +
specialization + community love.** The single greatest threat to that moat is the
ownership change itself being mishandled. Handled well (see Mrs. Mac's), the change becomes
a *story worth following*. Source:
[Keys Weekly – Key Largo restaurant maintains local flavor under new ownership](https://keysweekly.com/42/key-largo-restaurant-maintains-local-flavor-under-new-ownership/).

### Community sentiment read *(client to confirm against actual comments)*
- Expect a wave of **grief + nostalgia** tied to the closure ("end of an era," "we'll miss
  Jill & Morgan").
- Expect **protective skepticism** toward new ownership ("hope they don't ruin it," "is it
  the same?").
- This is **not a problem to spin past — it's the emotional energy to honor and convert.**
  The campaign's job is to answer the unspoken question — *"Is it still my Shrimp Shack?"*
  — with a confident, respectful **yes**.

### 🤝 Community Messaging Guidance (how to reassure regulars while building excitement)

The hardest part of this campaign is emotional, not creative. These are the rules:

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| **Honor the past first.** Thank the prior owners by name *(if cleared)* before saying a word about the future. | Don't act like the Shack is "new." It's *continuing.* |
| **Name the icons and promise to protect them** (pink shrimp, piña colada basket, Hook & Cook). Specificity = proof you "get it." | Don't over-modernize the visuals. Keep the rustic, real-Keys look. |
| **Use "we kept it" language**, not "we improved it." Improvements are bonuses, never the headline. | Don't lead with renovations, new branding, or "elevated dining." |
| **Invite regulars to co-own the relaunch** — "tell us your favorite dish," "share your Shack memory." | Don't ignore or delete nostalgic/skeptical comments — engage warmly. |
| **Reply to every early comment** in the owner's voice, warm and human. | Don't sound corporate. No press-speak on social. |
| **Let the food do the reassuring** — the first plate photo should look *exactly* like the one they remember. | Don't tease the menu and then change everything. Match expectations. |

**The trust sequence (recommended order):**
1. **Gratitude** — thank the past, acknowledge the closure honestly.
2. **Reassurance** — name the icons, promise to protect them.
3. **Invitation** — ask the community to share memories / favorites.
4. **Excitement** — countdown, sneak peeks, reopening date.
5. **Welcome home** — opening day as a community reunion, not a grand opening.

**Audiences & messages:**
- Regulars/locals → "Your seat's still here. Same shrimp, same soul."
- Returning visitors → "Yes — it's still open, and it's still *that* good."
- New visitors → "The Islamorada shrimp basket everyone tells you to try."
- Anglers → "Hook & Cook is back. Bring your catch."

**Channels:** Instagram (primary — vibe + reels), Facebook (primary for locals/regulars +
the emotional announcement reach), Google Business (the make-or-break "are they open?"
discovery + reviews), email/SMS for the prior mailing list *(if available)*.

**90-day arc:**
- **Days 1–30:** Honor + reassure + tease. Transition announcement, "Same Soul" content
  system, memory-collection campaign, reopening countdown. *(This proposal's 30-day plan.)*
- **Days 31–60:** Reopening week as a community homecoming; re-introduce signature dishes;
  launch review engine to rebuild fresh 5-star momentum under new ownership.
- **Days 61–90:** Optimize from dashboard data; layer Hook & Cook angler push and visitor
  acquisition; introduce premium video + light paid amplification on proven winners.

## 10. AI Automation Opportunities

Using the standard a.i. STaRR stack (see [brand guide](../_shared/brand-guide.md#5-standard-ai-automation-stack)):

| Workflow | What it does | Tools | Hours saved/wk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Content engine** | Generates monthly calendar + draft posts from the 5 pillars; routes to owner approval | ChatGPT/Claude + Google Sheets | ~5 |
| **Auto-posting** | Publishes approved posts/reels on schedule to IG + FB | Make.com + Meta API | ~3 |
| **Reopening countdown** | Auto-schedules the daily countdown + reopening-week sequence | Make.com + Sheets | ~1 |
| **Memory / UGC capture** | Pulls "Share Your Shack Memory" tags + mentions into a repost/feature queue | Make.com + Meta API + Sheets | ~2 |
| **Review generation** | Post-meal trigger sends SMS/email asking for a Google review (rebuilds fresh 5-star momentum under new ownership) | Make.com + SMS/email | ~2 |
| **Comment triage** | Flags nostalgic/skeptical comments to the owner for a personal reply; auto-suggests warm draft responses | Make.com + Meta API | ~2 |
| **Reporting** | Pulls weekly metrics into a live dashboard | Make.com + Sheets/Looker Studio | ~1 |

**Review generation system:** The relaunch *resets* the review momentum, so rebuilding
fresh, dated 5-star reviews under new ownership is mission-critical. Post-meal trigger
(QR on the check or manual) → timed SMS/email with a one-tap Google review link → routes
5-star intent public, flags <4-star privately for service recovery during the sensitive
early weeks.

**Comment-triage system (campaign-specific):** Because this launch is won or lost in the
comments, a Make.com flow watches IG/FB for emotional or skeptical comments, alerts the
owner, and drafts a warm, on-voice reply for one-tap approval — so no regular ever feels
unheard.

**Reporting dashboard:** One link showing followers, reach, top posts, memories collected,
reviews generated, and sentiment trend — refreshed automatically.

**Net effect:** ~15+ hours/week of manual marketing work removed while *increasing* output
to daily and keeping a human, personal tone where it matters most.

$2,500 + automation buildout (posting, reviews, comment triage, dashboard) $3,000 +

for itself with roughly **4 extra tables per day**. For a relaunch, the bigger ROI is
*risk reduction* — a botched reopening can cost a 15-year reputation; a well-run one
converts grief into a packed dining room on opening week.
