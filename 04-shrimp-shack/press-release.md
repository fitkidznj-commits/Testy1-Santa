## Beloved Islamorada Shrimp Shack to Reopen Under New Ownership — With a Promise to Keep Its "Keys Soul" Intact

### After nearly 15 years and a national *Diners, Drive-Ins & Dives* spotlight, the Overseas Highway landmark returns — same local shrimp, same signature dishes, same island spirit.

**ISLAMORADA, FL — [DATE]** — One of the Florida Keys' most beloved local seafood spots is
coming back. The **Islamorada Shrimp Shack**, the family-run institution at 81901 Overseas
Highway known for its fresh local Key West pink shrimp, its famous piña-colada-batter
shrimp basket, and its guest-favorite "Hook & Cook" program, will reopen under new

### About the Islamorada Shrimp Shack *(boilerplate — client to confirm)*

The Islamorada Shrimp Shack is a family-run seafood restaurant on the Overseas Highway in
Islamorada, Florida, serving fresh local Key West pink shrimp and Florida Keys seafood
classics in a laid-back, authentic island setting. A longtime local favorite featured on
Food Network's *Diners, Drive-Ins & Dives*, the Shack is known for its piña-colada-batter
shrimp basket, shrimp and grits, and its "Hook & Cook" program. Under new ownership as of
2026, the restaurant continues its long-standing tradition under the promise: *New owners.
Same Keys soul.* For hours, menu, and reservations, visit
[islamoradashrimpshack.com](http://www.islamoradashrimpshack.com/) or call [phone].
