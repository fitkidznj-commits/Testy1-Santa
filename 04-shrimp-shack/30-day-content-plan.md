## Sample Graphic Specs (art direction for Canva)

> a.i. STaRR can render these on the Shrimp Shack brand kit as a production add-on. Keep
> the look **rustic and real** — weathered wood, hand-lettering, salt-faded color — not
> slick or corporate. The community must recognize it instantly.

1. **Transition announcement graphic** — 1080×1350. Warm photo of the storefront / screen
   door. Hand-lettered banner: *"New Owners. Same Keys Soul."* Small subhead: *"A new
   chapter for the Islamorada Shrimp Shack."* Faded coral + sand palette, weathered-wood
   texture frame.

2. **"It's staying" reassurance series** — 1080×1080, repeatable template. Hero photo of
   one icon dish (piña colada basket, shrimp & grits, po'boy). Stamp-style overlay:
   *"STAYING. ✅"* Bold hand-stamp font, kraft texture, shrimp-coral accent.

3. **Reopening-date / countdown graphic** — 1080×1350, repeatable. Big chalkboard-style
   number ("7 DAYS," "3 DAYS," "TOMORROW"). Headline *"The screen door opens [DATE]."*
   Shrimp icon, Shack logo top, "📍 81901 Overseas Hwy" footer bar.

4. **"What dish first?" poll graphic** — 1080×1080. Four-quadrant dish photos with
   hand-drawn labels, center stamp *"You pick opening day."* Encourages comment voting.

5. **Hook & Cook revival card** — 1080×1350. Action photo of a fresh catch on the counter.
   Hand-lettered *"Hook & Cook is back."* Subhead: *"Bring your catch. We'll cook it."*
   Rope/twine graphic accent, weathered-wood frame.

6. **"Your seat's still here" welcome graphic** — 1080×1080. Empty picnic table / chair by
   the screen door at golden hour. Script overlay: *"Your seat's still here."* Used for the
   emotional reassurance posts and reopening week.

**Brand kit (recommended — warm/rustic, distinct from Whale Harbor's polish):** shrimp
coral `#E8743B`, weathered teal `#3A7D7B`, sand/kraft `#E8D9B5`, deep driftwood
`#4A3B2F`, off-white `#FBF7EE`. Hand-lettered/stamp headlines, clean sans for body,
weathered-wood and kraft-paper textures used throughout.
