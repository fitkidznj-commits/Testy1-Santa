# Project 10 — Michelle Lincoln Campaign Marketing & Communications Proposal

**Client:** Michelle Lincoln — Monroe County, Florida Keys *(local leader / re-election campaign)*
**Prepared by:** a.i. STaRR
**Goal:** Show how a.i. STaRR can help a local leader communicate, engage, and tell
her story more effectively — with a ready-to-run content system and AI-powered
campaign communications.

> **Tone note:** This is a marketing and communications proposal — positive,
> professional, and nonpartisan. It focuses on how to tell the candidate's story and
> serve constituents better. It is **not** opposition or attack content, and it does
> not put words, quotes, or policy positions in the candidate's mouth. Items needing
> the candidate's sign-off are flagged **"client to confirm."**

---

## Executive Summary

Michelle Lincoln is a recognized Florida Keys public servant: a Monroe County
Commissioner who has also served as County Mayor / Mayor Pro Tem, as President of the
Florida Association of Counties, and previously as a Marathon City Councilwoman and
Mayor *(roles and current title — client to confirm exact district and current
office)*. That is a strong, credible story of local leadership — but in a re-election
season, a strong record only matters if residents **hear it, understand it, and feel
included in it.**

The opportunity: turn a public-service record into a clear, consistent, community-first
communications presence. Most local campaigns post sporadically, react instead of plan,
and rely on a single volunteer's spare evenings. a.i. STaRR proposes a calm, organized
content system — daily-rhythm posting, resident-spotlight storytelling, event
promotion, and AI automation for volunteer coordination and reporting — so the
campaign communicates like a professional operation without burning out the team.

**Headline outcome:** A complete 30-day campaign content system live in 30 days,
consistent posting across Facebook and Instagram, a resident spotlight series that
centers the community, streamlined volunteer and event communication, and a simple
dashboard that shows what is reaching people — all built on public, nonpartisan
community priorities of the Florida Keys.

**Recommended package:** Growth ($3,000/mo), framed for a campaign budget and timeline.
See [`proposal.md`](proposal.md#11-pricing-recommendations).
