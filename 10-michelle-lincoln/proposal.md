## 4. Social Media Strategy

**Content pillars (the "5 C's"):**
1. **Community** — residents, neighborhoods, local life across the Keys (the heart of
   the feed).
2. **Connection** — the candidate showing up: events, meetings, listening sessions,
   accessible and human.
3. **Commitment** — general issue context residents care about (housing, insurance,
   environment, resilience), framed positively and **as approved by the campaign**.
4. **Credibility** — the public-service record and recognitions, told warmly, not
   boastfully *(facts client to confirm)*.
5. **Call to Action** — get involved, attend an event, vote, volunteer, share.

**Cadence (Growth tier):** ~1 feed post/day across Facebook + Instagram with 1–2
stories/day; 8–10 reels/month; geotag Keys locations (Marathon, Islamorada, Key Largo,
Key West); consistent campaign hashtag *(client to confirm, e.g. #LincolnForTheKeys)*
plus rotating local tags.

**Engagement plan:** reply to comments and messages quickly with friendly,
approved templates; reshare positive resident content; keep a strictly **positive,
nonpartisan** tone; never engage in attacks. A simple comment-moderation guideline
protects the brand and keeps the page welcoming.

## 10. AI Automation Opportunities

Using the standard a.i. STaRR stack (see
[brand guide](../_shared/brand-guide.md#5-standard-ai-automation-stack)). For a
campaign, automation handles the *logistics* so the team can focus on people. **Every
public-facing item is reviewed and approved by the campaign before it goes out.**

| Workflow | What it does | Tools | Hours saved/wk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AI content creation** | Drafts the monthly calendar + post copy from approved pillars/themes; routes to campaign for approval | ChatGPT/Claude + Google Sheets | ~5 |
| **Auto-posting** | Publishes approved posts/reels on schedule to FB + IG | Make.com + Meta API | ~3 |
| **Volunteer communication** | Sign-up form → CRM → welcome message → shift reminders → thank-you; segment by interest (canvass, events, phone, social) | Make.com + Sheets + email/SMS | ~4 |
| **Event promotion** | One event entry fans out to a post, a story, a reminder sequence, and a recap prompt automatically | Make.com + Sheets + Meta API + email/SMS | ~3 |
| **Resident spotlight intake** | Collects resident-story submissions + consent → drafts a spotlight → approval queue | Make.com + Forms + Sheets | ~2 |
| **Reporting dashboard** | Pulls weekly reach, growth, top posts, RSVPs, volunteer counts into one live view | Make.com + Sheets/Looker Studio | ~1 |

**AI-powered content creation:** a content engine drafts the calendar and post copy
from campaign-approved themes, so the team edits and approves instead of starting from a
blank page. Tone is locked to positive/nonpartisan; the candidate's voice and any
positions stay human-approved.

**Volunteer communication workflow:** a single "Get Involved" form captures supporters,
tags them by how they want to help, sends an instant friendly welcome, then delivers
shift reminders and thank-you notes automatically — so no volunteer is ever forgotten
and the organizer stops living in group texts.

**Event promotion system:** the campaign enters an event once (date, place, details);
automation generates the announcement post, an Instagram story, a reminder sequence
(email/SMS to RSVPs), and a "post your photos" recap prompt — turning each event into a
full mini-campaign without extra manual work.

**Reporting dashboard:** one link the candidate and manager can check anytime —
follower growth, weekly reach, top-performing posts, event RSVPs, volunteer sign-ups,
and list growth — refreshed automatically, explained in plain English.

**Net effect:** ~15+ hours/week of manual coordination removed while *increasing*
consistency — the campaign communicates like a professional operation on a local
budget.
