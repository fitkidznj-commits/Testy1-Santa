# Michelle Lincoln — Video Concepts & Sample Reels

Companion to [`proposal.md`](proposal.md). Three flagship video concepts plus short-form
reels.

> **Authenticity first.** For a campaign we recommend **real footage** of the candidate
> and community as the core. AI (HeyGen/Creatomate) is used for **editing speed,
> captioning, and recap assembly** — **not** synthetic likenesses or fabricated audio of
> the candidate. Every script line, fact, and on-screen claim is **client to confirm**
> and campaign-approved. Add the required **"Paid for by…" disclaimer**.

---

## Video Strategy (summary)

- **3 flagship films** anchor the campaign: an **Introduction** (who she is, why she
  serves), a **Community Engagement** film (showing up + listening), and a **Small
  Business** film (the local economy). Evergreen — used on the link hub, pinned posts,
  and as the spine for cut-down reels.
- **Short reels** carry the daily community rhythm (events, gratitude, spotlights).
- **AI acceleration:** templated captioned reels and fast event recaps assembled from a
  shared, real-footage clip library; auto-captioning for accessibility.

## Flagship Concept 1 — Introduction Video ("Why I Serve") · ~60–90s

**Goal:** Warmly introduce the candidate and her connection to the Keys. Credible,
human, not boastful.

| Scene | Visual (real footage) | Audio / VO (candidate, approved) | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (0–8s) | Candidate walking a familiar Keys spot at golden hour | "I've called the Keys home for [years]…" *(confirm)* | "Michelle Lincoln" |
| 2 (8–25s) | Candid moments: talking with neighbors, at a local spot | "…and serving this community has been the honor of my life." *(role — confirm)* | — |
| 3 (25–45s) | B-roll: water, neighborhoods, working waterfront, families | "We face real challenges — housing, insurance, protecting what makes this home." *(approved framing)* | "Local. Listening." |
| 4 (45–70s) | Candidate listening at an event; warm eye contact to camera | "I don't have all the answers — but I'll always show up and listen." | — |
| 5 (70–85s) | Logo over a Keys sunrise | VO: "Let's keep working for the Keys — together." | Logo + link + "Paid for by [committee]" |

## Flagship Concept 2 — Community Engagement Video ("Showing Up") · ~45–60s

**Goal:** Prove accessibility — the candidate out in the community, listening across the
Keys (Marathon, Islamorada, Key Largo, Tavernier, Key West).

- **Open:** Quick, real montage of the candidate at events, markets, meetings, docks —
  genuine handshakes and conversations.
- **Middle:** Short, unscripted soundbites from **consenting residents** ("I talked to
  her about ___") — real voices, approved clips only.
- **VO/text:** "Across every island, the same approach: show up, listen, work." *(client
  to confirm)*
- **Close:** "Have something to share? I want to hear it." → event/contact CTA + link +
  disclaimer.
- **Shot list:** event wide shots, candid 2-shots, resident soundbites (consent on
  file), island-sign B-roll for geography, warm natural light throughout.

---

## Flagship Concept 3 — Small Business Video ("The Heart of Our Economy") · ~45–60s

**Goal:** Connect the candidate to the local economy and the people who run it.

- **Open:** Sunrise over a working marina / a shop opening up.
- **Middle:** The candidate visiting 2–3 **consenting** local businesses (restaurant,
  charter/marina, home-services or shop) — real conversations, not staged ads.
- **Owner soundbites (approved):** what they love, what's hard, what they hope for.
- **VO/text:** "Small businesses are the Keys. A strong, resilient local economy keeps
  our community whole." *(approved framing — client to confirm)*
- **Close:** "Tag a local business you love" + link + disclaimer.
- **Note:** confirm each business consents to appear; this is community storytelling,
  not a paid endorsement of any business.

## Production Notes

- **One shoot day** (events + B-roll + candidate walk-and-talk) feeds all three
  flagship films **and** 8+ reels — efficient and authentic.
- a.i. STaRR edits the films, cuts the reels, auto-captions for accessibility, and
  assembles event recaps within ~24–48h using the AI-accelerated editing pipeline.
- **Consent + compliance:** signed releases for residents/businesses on camera; required
  **"Paid for by [committee]"** disclaimer on all published video per campaign counsel.
