## Resident Spotlight Series (concept)

**Idea:** The feed's heartbeat. Each spotlight features a real Keys resident, family,
volunteer, or small business — a short story about what they love about the Keys and
what they hope for its future. It centers the **community**, not the candidate, and
builds genuine connection.

**Format:** Carousel or short reel — a warm photo, 2–3 quote lines (with signed
consent), and a closing line tying to community. **Cadence:** ~1/week.

**Intake & consent:** A simple "Share your Keys story" form collects submissions +
photo/consent; AI drafts a clean spotlight; the campaign and the resident both approve
before publishing. (See the automation workflow in
[`proposal.md`](proposal.md#10-ai-automation-opportunities).)

**Spotlight template caption:**
> Meet [Name], [role/connection] in [town]. 🐠 "[Approved 1–2 sentence quote about life
> in the Keys / hopes for the community.]" Stories like [Name]'s are what this is all
> about. Know a neighbor we should feature? Tell us below. ⬇️
> *(Paid for by [committee].)*

## Sample Campaign Posts (templates — campaign approves all final copy)

**1 (CN/Reel — Intro):** Hi, I'm Michelle. 👋 I've been lucky to call the Keys home and
to serve this community [role — client to confirm]. This season I want to do more than
campaign — I want to **listen**. Follow along, tell me what matters to you, and let's
keep working for the Keys together. *(Paid for by [committee].)*

**2 (CM/Photo):** There's no place like the Keys. 🌅 [Location] never gets old. What's
the corner of the Keys that feels most like home to you? Tell me in the comments. ⬇️

**3 (CR/Photo):** Public service in the Keys isn't a title — it's showing up, year after
year, for the people who live here. [General record summary — client to confirm.] Want
the full story? Link in bio. 🔗

**6 (CT/Photo — issue context):** Families who keep the Keys running shouldn't be priced
out of living here. Housing affordability is one of the biggest challenges our community
talks about.* I want to hear it straight from you: what would make the biggest
difference for your family? ⬇️ *(Approved framing — client to confirm.)*

**7 (CA/Photo — volunteer):** Campaigns in the Keys are powered by neighbors, not big
budgets. 🙌 If you've got an hour, a skill, or just some energy, we'd love your help —
canvassing, events, phones, or sharing posts. Sign up here: [link].

**9 (CN/Photo — event):** Great morning at [event] in [town]. 🗣️ Thank you to everyone
who came out to talk story and share what's on your mind. This is the best part of the
job. Next gathering details coming soon.

**13 (CA/Event):** 📅 You're invited! Join us [day, date] at [place, town] for [event].
Come meet the team, ask questions, and share what matters to you. RSVP here: [link].
Light bites + good company. See you there!

**15 (CM/Photo — small business):** Small businesses ARE the Keys. 🛟 Today's shout-out
goes to [business] in [town] — [one warm line]. Tag a local business you love and let's
fill the comments with Keys gold. ⬇️

**19 (CN/Reel — event recap):** What a turnout. ❤️ Thank you to everyone who came to
[event]. Your questions, your ideas, your energy — this is what community looks like.
Couldn't make it? Here's what you missed (and what's next). 👇

**20 (CM/Photo — volunteers):** These folks showed up on a [day] to help their
community. 🙌 Our volunteers are the heart of this campaign — thank you doesn't cover
it. Want to join them? Link in bio.

**22 (CT/Carousel — resilience):** One road in, one road out. 🌀 Storm resilience and
infrastructure matter more here than almost anywhere.* Swipe for why it's a community
priority — and tell me what makes you feel prepared (or not). *(Approved framing —
client to confirm.)*

**24 (CA/Photo — specific ask):** We need [10 door-knockers / phone-bank help / yard-sign
hosts] this week! 📣 Even an hour helps. If you can pitch in, comment "I'm in" or sign up
at [link]. The Keys take care of the Keys.

**26 (CM/Photo — community):** From sunrise on the water to Friday-night lights, the Keys
have traditions you can't find anywhere else. 🎣 What's the one you'd never give up?
Comment below — I'm taking notes.

**30 (CN/Reel — recap):** 30 days of listening, showing up, and meeting incredible
neighbors. 🙏 Thank you, Keys. We're just getting started — here's how you can be part of
what's next: [link]. *(Paid for by [committee].)*

## Sample Graphic Specs (art direction for Canva)

> a.i. STaRR can render these on the approved campaign brand kit (colors/logo **client
> to confirm**) as a production add-on. Suggested palette if none exists: deep Keys teal
> `#0E5C63`, warm sand `#E8D9B5`, sunset coral `#E8743B`, clean navy `#13334C`, white —
> friendly, coastal, civic. Approachable sans headlines.

1. **Resident Spotlight card** — 1080×1350. Resident photo top 2/3, soft coral banner
   bottom with "Resident Spotlight," name/town, one quote line, small campaign logo.
   Consistent template so the series is instantly recognizable.

2. **Event announcement** — 1080×1080. Warm community image, semi-transparent navy
   panel, large "You're Invited," date/time/place, RSVP/link prompt, logo + disclaimer
   line.

3. **Volunteer / Get Involved** — 1080×1350. Photo of volunteers, bold "Join the Team,"
   3 ways to help (icons: door, phone, share), QR/link, disclaimer footer.

4. **Issue-context card** — 1080×1080. Clean, calm. A community priority headline (e.g.,
   "Affordable housing for the families who keep the Keys running") in teal on sand,
   one supporting line, "Share your view ⬇️." *(Wording client to confirm.)*

5. **Quote / gratitude card** — 1080×1080. Sunset Keys photo, short approved quote or
   "Thank you, Keys ❤️," signature-style name, logo + disclaimer.

6. **Event recap** — 1080×1350. 3–4 real event photos in a grid, "Thank you for coming
   out," brief recap line, "Next up: [event]" teaser.

*All graphics include the required **"Paid for by [committee]"** disclaimer per campaign
counsel.*
