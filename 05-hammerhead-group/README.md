## Executive Summary

Hammerhead Group does the kind of work that sells itself — *if people can see it.*
Painting and home remodeling is the most visual service a homeowner ever buys: the
whole pitch is "imagine your house, but better." The problem for almost every
contractor in the Keys is the same — the transformations live on a phone camera roll,
never get posted, and never turn into the next job.

a.i. STaRR fixes that with a content system built on a simple loop: **shoot the
transformation → reveal it on social → capture the homeowner who sees themselves in
it.** Before/after reveals, time-lapse paint jobs, and customer testimonials become a
daily stream of proof, and an AI lead-capture layer turns every "how much would my
house cost?" comment and DM into a logged, followed-up estimate request.

The signature move: **"JC the Hammerhead"** — a friendly hammerhead-shark mascot who
hosts the tips, teases the reveals, and gives the brand a face people remember in a
category full of forgettable logos. In the Keys, where the shark is local and the
name is built in, JC turns a contractor into a *character* — and characters get
followed, shared, and called.

**Headline outcome:** A complete 30-day content system live in 30 days, 3–5 posts/week
of transformation-driven proof, a before/after-to-estimate lead engine, and a live
dashboard showing leads captured — positioning Hammerhead Group as the most-trusted,
most-visible home-services brand in the Keys.

**Recommended package:** Growth ($3,000/mo) — the right fit for a single-brand local
contractor focused on lead volume. See
[`proposal.md`](proposal.md#11-pricing-recommendations).

## How This Content Converts Into Leads

This is the through-line of the whole system. Every piece of content has a job:

| Stage | Content does this | Lead mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| **Stop the scroll** | Before/after reveal or JC tip catches the eye | Reach → new local eyeballs |
| **Build the trust** | Time-lapses, testimonials, "here's how we'd do it" | Proof → "these are the real deal" |
| **Plant the thought** | Caption asks "what room would you transform first?" | Comments → warm intent signals |
| **Make the ask** | Every post ends in a CTA: free estimate, DM "QUOTE" | Click/DM → captured lead |
| **Catch + follow up** | AI logs the lead, auto-replies, books the estimate | Estimate booked → job won |

The before/after is the hook *and* the close — see
[`proposal.md`](proposal.md#10-ai-automation-opportunities) for the lead engine.
