## Sample Graphic Specs (art direction for Canva)

> a.i. STaRR renders these on the Hammerhead brand kit (with the JC mascot) as a
> production add-on.

1. **Before/After reveal carousel (signature layout)** — 1080×1350, 6 slides.
   *Slide 1:* the **AFTER** shot full-bleed + small "AFTER ✅" tag + JC peeking in a
   corner. *Slide 2:* the **BEFORE** (identical angle/lighting) + "BEFORE" tag.
   *Slide 3:* prep/in-progress. *Slide 4:* crew at work. *Slide 5:* hero "after"
   detail. *Slide 6:* contact slide — logo, "Free Estimate," phone, "DM QUOTE," JC
   illustration + "That's a JC-approved transformation." Consistent corner ribbon for
   before/after labels.

2. **Before/After split graphic (single image)** — 1080×1080. Vertical or diagonal
   split, BEFORE left / AFTER right with a sliding-line motif, "SWIPE" prompt removed
   (it's one image), bold "TRANSFORMED" band, JC mascot at the seam holding a paint
   roller. Use for testimonials pairing and ads.

3. **"JC's Tip" card** — 1080×1350. Illustrated JC on one side (pointing/holding a
   tool), big friendly headline ("Prep is 80% of the job"), 3 bullet takeaways, brand
   footer with handle + "Ask JC in the comments." Recurring template — only headline +
   bullets change.

4. **5-star review graphic** — 1080×1080. Dark navy card, large gold ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐, pulled
   quote in quotation marks, customer first name + town, small JC "approved" badge,
   "Read more — link in bio."

5. **"JC Approved" quality badge** — transparent PNG. Circular seal: hammerhead
   silhouette + hammer, "JC APPROVED — Built for the Keys, Painted to Last." Stamp it
   on after-shots, testimonials, and the website.

6. **Free-estimate CTA / link-in-bio cover** — 1080×1920 (Story). JC waving, "Let's
   hammer it out," "Free Estimate 👉 swipe up / link in bio," phone + service area.

**Brand kit (recommended — client to confirm):** deep ocean navy `#10324F`, steel
slate `#5A6B7B`, hammerhead grey `#9AA7B0`, high-vis safety orange `#F26A21` (CTAs +
JC accents), clean white. Bold condensed sans for headlines (construction-strong),
friendly rounded sans for JC's voice/tips. JC illustration: approachable, slightly
cartoon, hard hat optional, paint roller or hammer in hand.
