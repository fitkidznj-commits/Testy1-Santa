### What the research says actually converts
- **Before/after is the #1 persuasion format** in remodeling — it *shows* the
  transformation instead of describing it (ServiceTitan; WebFX).
- **Carousels get ~3x the engagement** of single images, and the proven layout is:
  *after-shot first* → prep → crew at work → final → contact slide (Benjamin Moore).
- **Time-lapse transformation videos** + a caption that asks a question ("what room
  would you start with?") drive comments and DMs (Benjamin Moore; Basecoat).
- **15-second video testimonials paired with the before/after** beat any copy you
  could write — social proof closes the deal (Benjamin Moore).
- **Short-form video builds trust fastest** in 2026, and homeowners now start their
  contractor search on Google + social + AI search before they ever call (WebFX).
- **Cadence:** post 3–5x/week, use Reels + Stories for reach, reply fast — visible
  engagement signals "this is a pro" (Benjamin Moore; Monday Digital).

### Customer segments
1. **Local year-round homeowners** — repaints, kitchen/bath, repairs; buy on
   reputation and word of mouth.
2. **Second-home & vacation-rental owners (often out of state)** — high budgets, hire
   remotely, decide on proof and responsiveness. *High-value target.*
3. **Realtors & property managers** — repeat referrers; pre-sale refreshes and
   turnover work.
4. **Insurance / storm-recovery jobs** — episodic, high-ticket, urgent.

### Competitor & category observations (client to confirm handles/metrics)
- Most Keys contractors run **thin, inconsistent social** — occasional job photos, no
  story, no character, no CTA, no follow-up. The bar is low and the opportunity is wide.
- Almost none have a **brand personality** — it's a sea of logos and stock-blue
  trucks. A memorable mascot (JC) is a genuine differentiator.
- Few systematically **capture and reuse before/afters** or solicit reviews — so
  reach, ranking, and trust are all being left on the table.

## JC the Hammerhead — The Brand Mascot

> The signature creative element. JC turns "a contractor" into "a character people
> follow." The name is built in — *Hammer*head — and the hammerhead shark is pure
> Florida Keys, so the mascot is local, ownable, and instantly on-brand.

**Who JC is:** JC ("the Hammerhead") is a friendly, sharp-dressed hammerhead shark —
the brand's foreman, host, and resident expert. Think a confident, likeable Keys local
who happens to be the best in the business and isn't shy about it. The hammer in his
name is the hammer in his hand: he *builds* and he *transforms.*

**Personality:**
- **Confident, never arrogant** — he's the pro you actually want in your house.
- **Warm + funny** — quick with a pun ("let's *hammer* out the details"), but the work
  is always serious.
- **Straight-talking** — explains paint, prep, and remodels in plain English; no
  upsell-y contractor jargon.
- **Local to the bone** — knows salt air, hurricane season, and what a Keys home needs.

**Voice:** First person, friendly, a little cheeky. *"I'm JC — and I've got opinions
about your trim."* Aligns with the brand guide's "confident, not corporate / clear over
clever" tone.

**How JC is used across content:**

| Use | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| **Educational tips** | "JC's Quick Tips" — JC explains a paint/remodel question in 20 seconds (carousel or reel). Builds authority + trust. |
| **Before/after reveals** | JC "presents" the transformation — peeks in on the before, swims in for the after reveal. The hook of the whole brand. |
| **Testimonials** | JC introduces a happy homeowner; the customer tells their story. JC = credibility wrapper around real proof. |
| **Brand voice** | JC narrates captions, Stories, and reel VO. Catchphrase sign-offs (see below). |
| **Mascot graphics** | Illustrated JC for tip cards, "JC Approved" quality badge, holiday/seasonal posts, profile/highlight covers. |

**Catchphrases (rotate, don't overuse):**
- *"Let's hammer it out."* (free estimate CTA)
- *"That's a JC-approved transformation."* (after-reveal stamp)
- *"Built for the Keys. Painted to last."*

**Production note:** JC exists as (1) an **illustrated mascot** for graphics/badges
(built in Canva on the brand kit) and optionally (2) a **lightweight animated/voiced
presenter** via HeyGen for reels. Start illustrated; add motion as an upsell.

## 4. Social Media Strategy

**Content pillars (the "5 T's"):**
1. **Transformations** — full-home and room makeover reveals (the brand's anchor).
2. **Painting Projects** — interior/exterior jobs, color stories, time-lapses.
3. **Before/After** — the dedicated reveal format (carousel + reel), JC-hosted.
4. **Testimonials & Trust** — customer stories, reviews, "why locals call us."
5. **Educational (JC's Tips)** — paint/remodel know-how, Keys-specific advice.

*(Pillars 1–3 overlap by design — "transformation" is the brand DNA; the split lets us
vary format and keep the feed fresh without losing the through-line.)*

**Cadence (Growth tier):** ~30 posts/mo (3–5/week feed) + daily Stories; 8–10
reels/mo. Geotag every post to the job's town (Key Largo, Tavernier, Marathon, Key
West, etc.); branded tag **#HammerheadTransformation** + rotating local tags.

**Before/after best-practice rules (baked into every reveal):**
- Carousel order: **after first**, then before, prep, crew at work, final, contact slide.
- Shoot before *and* after from the **identical angle, height, and lighting**.
- Every reveal caption ends in a CTA + a question to spark comments.

**Engagement:** Reply to every comment/DM within hours (AI-assisted templates), route
"how much / can you do mine?" straight into the lead engine, run a monthly
"#HammerheadTransformation — show us your before" UGC prompt.

## 10. AI Automation Opportunities

Using the standard a.i. STaRR stack (see
[brand guide](../_shared/brand-guide.md#5-standard-ai-automation-stack)). The headline
system is **lead capture from before/after content** — turning reveals into estimates.

| Workflow | What it does | Tools | Hours saved/wk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Content engine** | Generates monthly calendar + draft posts/captions from the 5 pillars; routes to approval | ChatGPT/Claude + Google Sheets | ~5 |
| **Auto-posting** | Publishes approved posts/reels on schedule to IG + FB | Make.com + Meta API | ~4 |
| **Before/after → lead capture** ⭐ | Detects comment/DM intent ("how much," "can you do mine," "quote") on reveal posts → auto-reply with estimate link → logs lead | Make.com + Meta API + Sheets | ~4 |
| **Estimate intake + follow-up** | "Get my free estimate" form/DM → CRM log → instant auto-reply → 3-step follow-up sequence → booked walkthrough | Make.com + Sheets + email/SMS | ~3 |
| **Review generation** | After job completion, triggers SMS/email Google-review request with one-tap link; routes 5-star public, <4-star privately | Make.com + SMS/email | ~2 |
| **Reporting** | Pulls weekly metrics into a live dashboard | Make.com + Sheets/Looker Studio | ~1 |

**The before/after lead engine (the core idea):**
Every reveal post is tagged in the system. When a viewer comments or DMs with buying
intent ("how much for a house like this?", "do you do exteriors?", "quote please"),
Make.com detects the keyword → fires an auto-reply *("Love it! JC will get you a free
estimate — drop your zip + project here 👉 [link]")* → captures the response into the
Google Sheets CRM → kicks off a follow-up sequence. **The content that wins attention
and the system that captures the lead are one connected loop.**

**Estimate intake system:** Single "Get my free estimate" link-in-bio + DM
autoresponder → leads logged in CRM → instant auto-reply → 3-step follow-up (Day 0,
Day 2, Day 5) → owner dashboard of open estimate requests.

**Review generation system:** Post-completion trigger (manual "job done" tag or
CRM status) → timed SMS/email asking for a Google review with a one-tap link.

**Reporting dashboard:** One link showing followers, reach, top before/after posts,
**leads captured, estimates booked,** and reviews generated — refreshed automatically.

**Net effect:** ~15+ hours/week of manual marketing removed while *increasing* output
to a daily proof-and-capture rhythm.
