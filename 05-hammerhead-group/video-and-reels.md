## Video Strategy

- **Short-form reels carry the daily proof** — before/after reveals, satisfying paint
  time-lapses, JC quick-tips, and 15-second testimonials. High volume, trend-driven,
  shot on phone (the crew can capture most of it on the job).
- **Before/after is the engine.** Research is clear: transformation video + a question
  caption drives comments and DMs, and short-form builds trust fastest in 2026
  (see [research findings](proposal.md#2-research-findings)). Every reveal reel ends in
  a lead CTA wired to the
  [before/after lead engine](proposal.md#10-ai-automation-opportunities).
- **JC gives the brand a face.** The tip series and reveal "hosting" make a contractor
  memorable in a category of forgettable logos.
- **AI acceleration:** **HeyGen** renders JC as an animated/voiced presenter for the
  tip series and reveals without a shoot; **Creatomate** turns a shared before/after
  photo library + captions into templated reels on autopilot.
- **Shoot discipline (the one rule that makes it all work):** every job, shoot the
  **before from the exact angle, height, and lighting** you'll shoot the after. No
  matched angle, no reveal.

## Premium Video Concepts

### 1. "See It Before You Sign It" — Hero Brand Film (60–75s)
The flagship. A single Keys home goes from tired to transformed — interior + exterior +
a room remodel — intercut with the crew, clean job sites, and happy owners. JC opens
and closes as host/VO. Cinematic handheld + a few drone exteriors. Lives on the
homepage, ads, and pinned social. Ends on the "JC Approved" stamp + free-estimate CTA.

### 2. "Meet JC, Your Foreman" — Mascot Intro Film (30–45s)
Introduces the brand personality. JC (HeyGen-rendered presenter or animated) walks
through what Hammerhead stands for — Keys-tough work, honest pricing, transformations
you can see. Establishes voice and catchphrases. Pinned to profile; used in onboarding
new followers and in the proposal deck.

### 3. "From Their Home" — Customer Story Film (60s)
One homeowner, start to finish: the problem ("we hated this kitchen / we live up north
half the year"), the process, the reveal, the relief. Real before/after woven through.
The highest-trust asset — built for the second-home and realtor audiences.

### 4. "Built for the Keys" — Why It Lasts Film (45s)
The differentiator film: salt air, hurricane season, UV — and the prep + materials that
stand up to them. Positions Hammerhead as the contractor who builds for *this* climate,
not a generic mainland repaint. JC narrates the "what we check" list. Strong for
out-of-town owners doing due diligence.
