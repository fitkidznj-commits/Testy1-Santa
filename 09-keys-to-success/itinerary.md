# KEYS TO SUCCESS — 3-Day Event Itinerary

Prepared by **a.i. STaRR**. The full hour-by-hour experience for the 10-guest cohort. Designed
to alternate **content** (workshops, hot-seats) with **connection** (boat, sandbar, dinners) so
the room bonds as fast as it learns. Pricing and inclusions in
[`proposal.md`](proposal.md#5-pricing-strategy); public teaser in
[`landing-and-copy.md`](landing-and-copy.md#section-5--itinerary-teaser).

> **Design principle:** every "work" block is followed by a "water or table" block. By the end
> of Day 1 they're a cohort; by the end of Day 3 they're a network.
>
> **To confirm:** final venue, charter vessel & capacity, chef partners, and sunset times
> (Keys sunsets run ~7:45–8:15pm in summer). Times below are a working template.

## Day 1 — Arrival & The Room

*Theme: land, settle, set your three-day goal, and meet the nine.*

| Time | What | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1:00–3:00pm | Private arrivals & check-in | Private estate | Car service from MIA staggered; welcome cocktail + cold towel on arrival |
| 3:00–3:30pm | Settle in / unpack | Guest suites | Welcome kit waiting in each room (booklet, gift, KTS name card) |
| 3:30–4:00pm | Welcome circle | Waterfront deck | Host welcome, the three-day promise, the one rule: the room stays in the room |
| 4:00–5:30pm | **Opening workshop — "Your Key"** (mentor-led) | Workshop space | Each guest names the single outcome they want from three days; mentor frames the arc |
| 5:30–6:30pm | Golden-hour reset | Pool / dock | Open time, swim, change for dinner |
| 6:30–7:15pm | Sunset welcome cocktails | Waterfront deck | Sponsor spirits moment (Title/Experience partner); first real mingling |
| 7:15–9:30pm | **Private Chef's Welcome Dinner** | Estate chef's table | Multi-course local seafood; each guest gives a 90-second "who I am / what I'm building" |
| 9:30–10:30pm | Fireside & rum | Fire pit / dock | Unstructured. The night the cohort actually forms. Optional cigar/spirits pairing (sponsor) |

## Day 2 — The Water

*Theme: the signature day. Charter out, sandbar at noon, and a sunset hot-seat where the room
goes to work on each other's businesses.*

| Time | What | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7:30–8:30am | Sunrise coffee & light breakfast | Waterfront deck | Optional sunrise walk / dock yoga |
| 8:30–9:30am | **Morning workshop — "The Playbook"** (mentor-led) | Workshop space | Mentor teaches their core framework; sets the question each guest carries onto the water |
| 9:30–10:00am | Board & shove off | Marina | Private charter; captain's briefing; cohort photo on the bow |
| 10:00am–12:00pm | **On the water** | Open Atlantic | Cruise / light offshore fishing; small-group conversations seeded by the morning's question |
| 12:00–2:30pm | **The Sandbar Experience** | Offshore sandbar | Anchor up; floating lunch + spread; sponsor beverage moment; the relaxed, unforgettable centerpiece — deals start here |
| 2:30–3:30pm | Cruise back | Open water → marina | Wind-down; sun, music, conversation |
| 3:30–4:30pm | Return & refresh | Estate | Showers, rest, reset |
| 4:30–6:30pm | **Hot-Seat Session I** (3 guests) | Workshop space | Each hot-seat guest brings their biggest challenge; mentor + room work it live; action items captured |
| 6:30–7:15pm | Sunset on the dock | Waterfront | Cocktails; decompress before dinner |
| 7:15–9:45pm | **Private Chef's Dinner II** | Beachfront / off-site private dining | The marquee meal — beachfront chef's table; a sponsor "experience moment"; seating rotated so everyone connects |
| 9:45–11:00pm | Late-night table | Lounge / fire pit | Optional. The honest, off-record conversations the room remembers |

## Day 3 — The Unlock

*Theme: turn three days into a plan and a network you keep.*

| Time | What | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8:00–9:00am | Breakfast | Waterfront deck | Relaxed; informal mentor table |
| 9:00–10:30am | **Hot-Seat Session II** (remaining guests) | Workshop space | Same format; every guest gets the room's full attention before they leave |
| 10:30–10:45am | Break | Deck | Coffee, fresh air |
| 10:45am–12:15pm | **Build Your Plan** (mentor-facilitated) | Workshop space | Each guest writes a 90-day action plan tied to their Day-1 "key"; partners up with an accountability peer |
| 12:15–1:30pm | Networking lunch | Waterfront deck | Structured intros: "what I can offer the room / what I need" round |
| 1:30–2:30pm | **Closing mentor session — "Keys to Keep"** | Workshop space | Mentor's parting frameworks; commitments read aloud; cohort directory + private group activated |
| 2:30–3:00pm | Cohort photo & gifts | Waterfront | Group portrait; departure gift; recap-film B-roll |
| 3:00–4:30pm | Open afternoon | Estate / dock | Rest, swim, or 1:1 time. **Founder's Table 1:1 mentor sessions** slotted here |
| 4:30–5:30pm | Final reset | Suites | Change for the farewell dinner |
| 5:30–6:15pm | Sunset farewell cocktails | Waterfront deck | The last golden hour together; sponsor send-off moment |
| 6:15–8:30pm | **Farewell Dinner** | Estate chef's table | Each guest shares one commitment + one offer to the room; host closes; the network is sealed |
| 8:30pm onward | Departures / final night | Estate | Late departures and overnight stays; cars to MIA arranged for next morning |

## Networking Designed In (not left to chance)

| Touchpoint | How connection is engineered |
|---|---|
| **Day 1 dinner** | 90-second self-intros — everyone is "seen" the first night |
| **Sandbar** | Unstructured, relaxed, no agenda — where real rapport forms |
| **Rotated seating** | Every dinner reseats guests so all ten genuinely connect |
| **Hot-seats** | Guests solve each other's problems — the fastest trust-builder there is |
| **Accountability pairs** | Day-3 pairing keeps the relationship alive past the event |
| **Cohort directory + private group** | Activated Day 3 so the network outlives the three days |
