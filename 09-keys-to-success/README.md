# Project 9 — KEYS TO SUCCESS: Luxury Mastermind Launch Plan

**Experience:** KEYS TO SUCCESS — a 3-day, 10-guest luxury leadership mastermind in the Florida Keys
**Prepared by:** a.i. STaRR · Islamorada, FL
**Goal:** Launch and position the most exclusive leadership experience in the Florida Keys — 10 guests, 3 days, one elite mentor, total access.

---

## Executive Summary

**KEYS TO SUCCESS** is a high-ticket, application-only mastermind built around a simple promise: ten serious operators, one world-class mentor, three uninterrupted days in the Florida Keys — and the kind of access money usually can't buy. No stage. No 500-person ballroom. No selfie line. Just a small room, a fast boat, a sandbar at noon, and a mentor who actually answers your hardest question.

The opportunity: the high-ticket mastermind market is crowded with hotel-ballroom events priced $20K–$100K/year that all feel the same (see [`proposal.md`](proposal.md#2-the-market--why-now)). What almost none of them own is *place*. The Florida Keys give KEYS TO SUCCESS an unfair advantage — a setting that is itself the differentiator. We pair the proven high-ticket mastermind format (small vetted cohort, elite mentor, application gate) with a destination that turns a business retreat into a story guests tell for years: the boat day, the sandbar, the sunset dinner, the deal that started over rum.

**Headline outcome:** A fully launched, on-brand, application-only mastermind — brand identity, landing page, mentor-outreach engine, sponsorship program, pricing, and a complete hour-by-hour 3-day itinerary — ready to take applications and fill 10 seats.

**Recommended positioning:** Ultra-exclusive, single-mentor, single-cohort. Seat price anchored at **$25,000** (Inner Circle) with a **$45,000** Founder's tier. Full pricing logic in [`proposal.md`](proposal.md#5-pricing-strategy).
