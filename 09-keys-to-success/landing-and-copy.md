**Headline:** This isn't a conference. It's a room.

**Body:**
Most high-ticket events put you in a ballroom with five hundred strangers and a speaker on a
distant stage. You leave with a notebook and a tote bag.

KEYS TO SUCCESS does the opposite. Ten vetted guests. One elite mentor. Three days in the

## Section 8 — FAQ

**Where is it held?**
The Upper Florida Keys (Islamorada area) — a private estate with a charter on the water. Exact
venue is shared with invited guests. *(Venue to confirm.)*

**How many people are in each cohort?**
Ten. Always. It's a hard cap — the intimacy is the entire point.

**Who is the mentor?**
A single elite mentor leads each cohort — an entrepreneur, operator, coach, athlete, or
author. We announce the mentor for each cohort; invited applicants know before they commit.

**What's included in the investment?**
Three nights of private accommodation, all meals and beverages, the full charter and sandbar
day, two private chef's dinners, all mentor sessions, ground transport from Miami, your
welcome kit, and post-event cohort access. Founder's Table adds a private 1:1 and a 90-day
follow-up.

**What's not included?**
Airfare to Miami. Everything from the airport onward is handled.

**How does the application work?**
Apply for free in about five minutes. We review personally, interview finalists, and invite
ten guests. No payment until you're invited.

**What's the deposit and refund policy?**
A $5,000 deposit holds your seat once invited (non-refundable). Balance is due 30 days out.
Your seat is transferable to a vetted substitute up to 14 days before the event.

**Is this tax-deductible?**
Many guests treat it as a business expense — confirm with your accountant. *(Not tax advice.)*

**Can I bring my team / a partner?**
Seats are individual and vetted. If you'd like to nominate someone for a separate seat, tell
us in your application.
