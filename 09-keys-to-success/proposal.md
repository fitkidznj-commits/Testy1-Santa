## 1. Executive Summary

KEYS TO SUCCESS is an application-only luxury mastermind: **10 guests, 3 days, one elite
mentor, total access**, set in the Florida Keys. It takes the proven high-ticket mastermind
format — small vetted cohort, headline mentor, hard-to-get-in application — and gives it
something almost no competitor owns: a place that *is* the product. The boat day, the
offshore sandbar, the private chef's table at sunset — these turn a leadership retreat into
the most memorable three days of a guest's year.

This proposal covers everything needed to launch: brand identity, marketing plan, pricing
strategy, mentor outreach, sponsorship, AI automation, and the action plan. The companion
files deliver the public-facing copy ([`landing-and-copy.md`](landing-and-copy.md)) and the
full hour-by-hour experience ([`itinerary.md`](itinerary.md)).

## 2. The Market & Why Now

### What we found (research)

- **High-ticket masterminds are an established, premium category.** Marquee programs price
  membership at **$20,000–$50,000/year** (War Room) and **$25,000–$100,000/year** (Genius
  Network); exclusive expert-led groups can run **$5,000–$25,000/month** at the top end.
  This validates a five-figure seat price for a vetted, mentor-led experience.
- **Exclusivity is the product.** The most coveted groups are deliberately tiny and vetted —
  e.g., hyper-exclusive platinum groups of ~12 vetted executives in a single room. Small +
  screened = status. Our 10-guest hard cap is on-trend, not a limitation.
- **Application gates are standard at the top.** Premium masterminds qualify and screen
  rather than sell open seats. Applying is itself part of the prestige.
- **The Florida Keys are a proven luxury-experience market.** Islamorada is positioned as the
  "Sportfishing Capital of the World," with luxury yacht charters (e.g., the *Four Aces* out
  of Robbie's), private/multi-day charters, sandbar excursions, and a fine-dining scene
  (Chef Michael's, Green Turtle Inn est. 1947, Morada Bay) — all the raw ingredients for a
  destination mastermind.

*Sources:* [War Room / Genius Network pricing — Business Builder Camp](https://businessbuildercamp.com/how-much-does-a-business-mastermind-group-cost/),
[The Success Alliance — mastermind fee setting](https://www.thesuccessalliance.com/blog/mastermindgroupfeesetting/),
[GrowthMentor — top mastermind groups 2025](https://www.growthmentor.com/blog/mastermind-groups/),
[Luxury Presence — 2025 Collective Mastermind](https://www.luxurypresence.com/blogs/lessons-collective-mastermind-2025/),
[Robbie's of Islamorada — Four Aces luxury charter](https://www.robbies.com/captains-bios/four-aces.htm),
[Island Adventures — private Islamorada charter](https://www.goislandadventures.com/islamorada-sandbar-posts/private-charter-islamorada-beauty/),
[Charter Index — Florida Keys luxury yacht charter](https://www.charterindex.com/destinations/east-coast-united-states/florida-keys).

### The gap we exploit

Most high-ticket masterminds happen in interchangeable hotel ballrooms — the *content* is
premium but the *experience* is forgettable. KEYS TO SUCCESS makes the setting inseparable
from the value. You cannot screenshot the sandbar at noon. That is the moat.

> **Positioning insight:** Competitors sell access to a mentor. We sell access to a mentor
> *plus three days in a place that makes the access unforgettable.* Same proof point as the
> Whale Harbor playbook — win on the *combination* nobody else assembles.

**To confirm:** final venue (private estate vs. resort buyout), charter vessel and capacity,
and mentor availability/fee — all affect the cost base and the seat price below.

## 3. Brand Identity

### Name rationale — "KEYS TO SUCCESS"

A double meaning that does the heavy lifting:
1. **The Keys** — the literal place. Florida Keys. Saltwater, sportfishing heritage, the
   most aspirational waters in the U.S.
2. **The keys to success** — the unlock. The frameworks, the mentor's playbook, the room of
   peers who become the network that changes a guest's trajectory.

It is instantly memorable, owns its location, and frames the value (the *unlock*) without
sounding like a webinar. Clear over clever — exactly the house voice.

### Positioning statement

> **The most exclusive leadership experience in the Florida Keys.** Ten operators. One elite
> mentor. Three days that don't exist anywhere else. You don't register — you apply.

**One-liner:** *Where serious people go to get the keys.*

### Voice & tone

Built on the a.i. STaRR voice (confident, warm, Keys-authentic, clear over clever) and
dialed up for a luxury audience:

- **Confident and quiet.** Luxury whispers. No exclamation points, no hype stack, no
  countdown-timer energy. The scarcity is real, so we state it plainly.
- **Earned, not sold.** We describe the experience and the caliber of the room; we let the
  reader qualify themselves.
- **Keys-authentic.** Saltwater, sunsets, captains, the catch — never cheesy-touristy.
- **Specific.** "10 seats. 3 days. 1 mentor." Numbers signal precision and scarcity.

**Words we like:** room, access, unlock, captain, table, cohort, invitation, the catch.
**Words we avoid:** guru, hustle, 10X, "limited time only," ninja, masterclass-as-a-funnel.

### Visual direction / brand kit

| Element | Direction |
|---|---|
| **Palette** | Deep ocean navy `#0B2A3A` · twilight teal `#0E5E6F` · warm sand `#E8DCC4` · sunset gold `#C8922A` · off-white `#F7F4EC` |
| **Typography** | Display: a high-contrast serif (e.g., Canela / Ivar feel) for headlines. Body: a clean humanist sans (e.g., Söhne / Inter) for clarity. |
| **Photography** | Cinematic, low-volume, golden-hour. Open water from the bow, a single boat on glass-flat water, a sandbar from above, candlelit chef's table, no crowds. Aspirational solitude, not party-crowd. |
| **Texture & motion** | Generous negative space. Slow, filmic motion (a wake unfurling, ice in a glass, sun dropping). Restraint = luxury. |
| **Do not** | Stock-photo handshakes, neon CTAs, stacked badges, before/after screenshots. |

### Logo concept

- **Primary mark:** A minimalist key whose teeth form a subtle **island/wave silhouette** —
  the "key" and "the Keys" fused into one mark. Single-line, engravable, works in gold foil
  on navy.
- **Monogram:** A circular "KTS" seal for stamps, embossing, and the wax-seal feel on
  physical invitations.
- **Wordmark:** "KEYS TO SUCCESS" set in the display serif, wide letter-spacing, with the
  subline *"FLORIDA KEYS · BY INVITATION"* in small caps.
- **Usage:** Gold-on-navy as the hero lockup; sand-on-navy and navy-on-off-white as
  secondaries. Built as a Canva brand kit for collateral (invitations, itinerary booklet,
  name cards, boat flag, dinner menus).

## 4. Marketing Plan

The marketing job here is the inverse of most a.i. STaRR projects: we are **not** chasing
volume. We need ~10 highly qualified applicants per cohort (plus a waitlist). The goal of
every channel is to make the right person feel *"this was built for me — and I might not get
in."*

### Strategy: scarcity-led, referral-first

| Audience | Where they are | Message |
|---|---|---|
| Founders / CEOs ($1M–$50M) | LinkedIn, peer networks, existing masterminds | "Trade the ballroom for a boat. 10 seats." |
| Coaches / experts scaling | Instagram, podcasts, email lists | "The room that becomes your network." |
| High performers (athletes, creators) | Mentor's audience, private referrals | "Access you can't buy on a stage." |

### Channels (in priority order)

1. **The mentor's audience (primary).** The headline mentor's email list / following is the
   single best source of qualified applicants. Co-branded announcement + application link.
   (See outreach strategy, §6.)
2. **Private referral & nomination.** "Know someone who belongs in the room? Nominate them."
   Existing guests and partners refer — referral is the most luxury-consistent channel.
3. **Cinematic content drops (organic).** A short brand film + 6–8 reels carrying the *vibe*
   (boat on glass water, sandbar at noon, candlelit table). Goal: desire, not clicks.
   Delivered as concepts/specs; rendered via Canva/HeyGen as a production add-on.
4. **LinkedIn thought-leadership** from the mentor and host, seeding the "ballroom vs. boat"
   contrast and dropping the application link.
5. **Targeted paid (light, optional).** Lookalike of the mentor's buyers + retargeting site
   visitors. Small budget — the application gate does the filtering, not the ad spend.
6. **Earned / PR.** "Most exclusive leadership experience in the Florida Keys" is a story.
   Pitch business + luxury-travel press and relevant podcasts.

### Campaign arc (per cohort)

| Phase | Weeks out | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| **Tease** | 8–6 | Brand film + "something is coming to the Keys." Open waitlist (email capture only). |
| **Open** | 6–4 | Applications open. Mentor announcement. Scarcity stated plainly: 10 seats. |
| **Qualify** | 4–2 | Review applications, interview finalists, extend invitations. |
| **Fill & confirm** | 2–0 | Deposits collected, seats confirmed, pre-trip concierge begins. |
| **Amplify** | post-event | Recap film + guest testimonials become the marketing for the *next* cohort. |

The post-event recap film is the most important asset of all — it sells the next cohort and
justifies the price. Capture rights are built into the guest agreement (to confirm).

## 5. Pricing Strategy

This is a **high-ticket, value-anchored** offer. We follow the framework principle —
*lead with value, always show the math* (see [pricing framework](../_shared/pricing-framework.md#4-pricing-principles))
— scaled to a luxury experience rather than a monthly retainer.

### What justifies the investment

A guest is not buying three days of content. They are buying:
- **Proximity** — direct, unfiltered access to an elite mentor in a 10-person room (vs.
  speaking-from-a-stage to 500).
- **The room** — 9 vetted peers who become a lifelong network. In high-ticket masterminds,
  the peer network is consistently rated the #1 ROI driver.
- **The setting** — a fully hosted luxury experience (private estate, charter, private chefs)
  that would cost a guest $8K–$15K to assemble alone, and they *couldn't* assemble the mentor
  or the room at any price.
- **The unlock** — frameworks and a personalized action plan they implement the next Monday.

### Tiers

| Tier | Seats | Investment | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Inner Circle** | 7 | **$25,000** | The core cohort — full experience, all access. |
| **Founder's Table** ⭐ | 3 | **$45,000** | Guests who want the closest access: private 1:1 mentor session, the seat beside the mentor at every dinner, and a 90-day post-event follow-up call. |

*10 seats total. Anchored against the market's $20K–$100K/year band — priced as a single
flagship experience, not an annual membership. Figures **to confirm** against final venue,
charter, and mentor costing.*

### What's included (both tiers)

| Included | Detail |
|---|---|
| 3 days / 3 nights | Private luxury accommodation, all on-property |
| All meals & beverages | Including 2 private chef dinners + the sandbar spread |
| The boat day | Full private charter + offshore sandbar experience |
| Mentor sessions | Group workshops + facilitated hot-seats (see [itinerary](itinerary.md)) |
| Welcome kit | Branded itinerary booklet, KTS gift, name cards, dinner menus |
| Pre-trip concierge | Travel coordination, dietary/preferences intake, packing guide |
| Post-event | Cohort directory + private group; recap film access |
| Ground transport | Airport (MIA/Miami) transfers and all on-island transport |

**Founder's Table adds:** a private 1:1 mentor strategy session, seat-of-honor at every
dinner, a premium suite, and a 90-day post-event follow-up call.

### Deposit & payment

| Term | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Application** | Free. No payment until invited. |
| **Deposit to hold a seat** | **$5,000** non-refundable, due within 5 business days of invitation. |
| **Balance** | Due 30 days before the event. |
| **Payment options** | Pay in full, or 3 monthly installments after deposit (to confirm with finance/processor). |
| **Transfer policy** | Seat transferable to a vetted substitute up to 14 days out; non-refundable thereafter. |

**ROI framing (for the application/sales conversation):** if the room produces one
introduction, one hire, one partnership, or one reframed decision worth more than $25K — and
for this caliber of guest it routinely does — the experience pays for itself before the boat
leaves the dock. We frame the seat as *the cheapest line item in a year that it changes.*

## 6. Mentor / Speaker Outreach Strategy

The mentor is the single most important asset. The right name makes the cohort sell itself;
the wrong one makes it a hard sell at any price.

### Target mentor profiles

| Type | Why they fit | What we offer them |
|---|---|---|
| **Entrepreneur / founder** | Built and exited; credibility with the founder cohort | A premium, low-effort, high-prestige format + revenue share/honorarium |
| **Business leader / executive** | Operating wisdom, gravitas | Thought-leadership platform + curated room of buyers |
| **Coach / expert** | Already monetizes an audience; warm list to fill seats | Turnkey luxury delivery — we run everything, they show up brilliant |
| **Pro athlete** | Performance/mindset angle, marquee draw | Aspirational halo + light time commitment |
| **Bestselling author** | Built-in audience + framework to teach | Book/IP amplification + an intimate flagship event |

**Ideal first mentor:** an entrepreneur or coach with (a) a genuinely engaged audience to
help fill the cohort, (b) real operating credibility, and (c) the temperament for an intimate
room (not a stage performer). The audience is the multiplier.

### Outreach approach

1. **Build a 20-name target list**, tiered A/B/C by draw and audience size.
2. **Warm path first** — find a mutual introduction before any cold outreach. One referral
   beats fifty cold emails in this world.
3. **Lead with prestige and ease, not money.** The pitch is: *we've built the most exclusive
   leadership experience in the Keys, and we want you to be its mentor — we handle
   everything.*
4. **Offer a clear deal structure:** honorarium **or** revenue share on seats sold through
   their audience (model both; to confirm based on draw).
5. **Make it tangible fast** — send the brand one-pager + draft itinerary so they can picture
   themselves on the boat, at the table.
6. **Single decision, single ask** — a 20-minute call. Don't over-email.

### Sample outreach message

> **Subject:** The most exclusive room in the Florida Keys — and we want you to lead it
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> I'll keep this short because your time is the whole point.
>
> We're launching **KEYS TO SUCCESS** — a 3-day, 10-person leadership mastermind in the
> Florida Keys. Ten vetted operators, one elite mentor, total access. A private estate, a
> day on the water, a sandbar at noon, and two private chef's dinners. No ballroom, no stage,
> no selfie line. The setting *is* the experience.
>
> We'd like you to be the mentor for the first cohort.
>
> Here's the part that matters: **you just show up and be brilliant.** We handle the brand,
> the applications, the vetting, the logistics, the chefs, the boat — everything. You lead
> three workshops and a few hot-seats, and you'll be in one of the most beautiful rooms
> you've ever taught.
>
> We're flexible on structure — honorarium or a revenue share on seats filled through your
> audience, whichever fits how you like to work.
>
> Could I send you the one-pager and grab 20 minutes this week? I think you'll see the room
> immediately.
>
> [Signature]

## 7. Sponsorship Strategy

Sponsors offset the cost base and elevate the experience — but only the *right* brands, and
only in ways that feel like a gift to the guest, never an ad. Ten ultra-high-net-worth
guests in a room for three days is a category-defining audience for the right partner.

### Principles

- **Curated, not cluttered.** Maximum 3–4 sponsors per cohort. Exclusivity protects the
  brand for both sides.
- **Category exclusivity** — one spirits brand, one watch/jewelry, one private aviation, etc.
- **Sponsorship = experience enhancement.** A sponsor *gives* the guest something memorable
  (the rum at the sandbar, the watch on each pillow, the jet home), not a banner.

### Sponsor tiers

| Tier | Investment (to confirm) | What the sponsor gets |
|---|---|---|
| **Title Partner** | $50,000+ | "KEYS TO SUCCESS, presented by [Brand]." Category exclusivity, logo on all collateral + recap film, a branded experience moment (e.g., the welcome dinner), a guest-list dinner seat, full guest data-capture rights (opt-in), and content rights. |
| **Experience Partner** | $20,000–$35,000 | Sponsors a signature moment — the boat day, the sandbar spread, or a chef's dinner. Branded touch within that moment, recap-film inclusion, product placement, mention in collateral. |
| **Gift Partner** | $7,500–$15,000 | Premium product in the welcome kit / on each pillow (spirits, watch, apparel, wellness). Inclusion in the gift guide + a tasteful social mention. |

### Ideal sponsor categories

Luxury spirits & rum, premium watches/jewelry, private aviation & yacht brokerage, luxury
auto, high-end wellness/recovery, premium apparel, and wealth/private-banking partners. Each
maps naturally to a moment in the [itinerary](itinerary.md).

### The sponsor pitch

> "Ten vetted founders and leaders, three days, total attention, zero competition for their
> focus. Your brand isn't a logo on a banner — it's the rum they remember from the sandbar,
> the watch they wear home. The most premium 10-person audience in the Keys."

## 8. AI Automation Opportunities

Using the standard a.i. STaRR stack (see
[brand guide](../_shared/brand-guide.md#5-standard-ai-automation-stack)), automation runs the
high-touch experience *without* feeling automated — concierge polish at scale.

| Workflow | What it does | Tools | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Application intake & scoring** | Web application form → logged in Sheets CRM → AI drafts a fit-score + summary for each applicant against ideal-guest criteria | Make.com + Sheets + Claude/ChatGPT | Faster, consistent vetting |
| **Application status sequence** | Auto-acknowledge on submit; route to "interview / invite / waitlist / decline"; send the right branded email at each step | Make.com + email/SMS | Every applicant feels handled |
| **Waitlist engine** | Captures over-demand; auto-notifies waitlisted applicants the moment a seat opens; ranks by fit score | Make.com + Sheets + email/SMS | Never lose a qualified lead; manufactured scarcity is real |
| **Invitation & deposit** | On invite, sends the offer + payment link; tracks the 5-day deposit window; nudges before expiry; releases seat if unpaid | Make.com + Sheets + payment processor | Clean seat management |
| **Pre-trip concierge** | Drip of logistics (travel, dietary intake, packing guide, mentor prep) timed to the event date; collects guest preferences | Make.com + email/SMS + Sheets | White-glove without manual chasing |
| **Follow-up & nurture** | Post-event: thank-you, recap-film delivery, testimonial request, cohort-directory access, and a sequence inviting referrals/next cohort | Make.com + email/SMS | Turns guests into the next cohort's recruiters |
| **Mentor-outreach tracker** | Logs the 20 target mentors, tracks status, AI-drafts personalized follow-ups | Make.com + Sheets + Claude/ChatGPT | Keeps outreach moving |

**Net effect:** an application-only, concierge-grade experience that *feels* personally
hand-run while the routing, scoring, reminders, and follow-up happen automatically — exactly
the a.i. STaRR promise applied to a luxury audience.
