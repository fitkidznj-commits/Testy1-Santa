# Ari Landscaping — Website Mockup (Section-by-Section)

Companion to [`proposal.md`](proposal.md). This is a **conversion-focused, local-SEO
website** described section-by-section with wireframe-style layout notes and
ready-to-use sample copy. a.i. STaRR builds the live site in the production phase.

> Bracketed items and anything marked *(client to provide / confirm)* are placeholders —
> logo, photos, exact service list, service area, phone, hours, and license # come from
> the client. See [`README.md`](README.md#required-client-inputs).

**Recommended brand kit:** deep palm green `#2E5E3A`, fresh leaf green `#6FAE5B`,
warm sand `#E8DDC7`, charcoal text `#222`, white. Clean sans headlines; large,
high-quality photos do the heavy lifting. *(Final palette tuned to Ari's logo — client to provide.)*

**Global elements (every page):**
- **Sticky header:** [Ari logo] · Home · Services ▾ · Gallery · Reviews · About ·
  **[ Get a Free Estimate ]** button (high-contrast) · **click-to-call phone** (mobile).
- **Sticky mobile footer bar:** `📞 Call` | `💬 Text` | `Free Estimate` — always visible.
- **Footer:** NAP (Name/Address/Phone — *client to confirm*), service area list,
  service links, hours, "Licensed & Insured" badge, social links, copyright.
- **Trust strip** repeated site-wide: ⭐ [X.X] Google rating · Licensed & Insured ·
  [Years] in [Area] · Free Estimates *(client to confirm figures)*.

### 7a. Lawn Maintenance / Mowing — sample copy
- **H1:** Lawn Maintenance in [Service Area]
- **Intro:** A clean, healthy lawn without lifting a finger. We mow, edge, trim, and blow
  on a reliable weekly or biweekly schedule — so your yard always looks cared for.
- **What's included:** Mowing · Edging · String-trimming · Blowing/cleanup · Optional
  fertilization & weed control *(client to confirm)*.
- **How it works:** 1) Free estimate → 2) Pick weekly or biweekly → 3) We show up like clockwork.
- **FAQ:** *"How often should I mow in South Florida?"* / *"Do you offer one-time cuts?"*

### 7b. Landscape Design & Installation — sample copy
- **H1:** Landscape Design & Installation in [Service Area]
- **Intro:** Dreaming of a yard that turns heads? We design and install landscapes built
  for South Florida — using salt-tolerant, hurricane-smart plants that actually thrive here.
- **What's included:** Design consultation · Plant selection (natives like gumbo-limbo,
  seagrape, sabal palm) · Bed prep · Installation · Cleanup.
- **Why it ranks/sells:** ties in local expertise — coastal yards need wind- and salt-
  tolerant natives ([Florida Keys Treasures](https://floridakeystreasures.com/native-plants-take-stand-hurricanes/)).

### 7c. Trimming & Pruning (incl. palms) — sample copy
- **H1:** Tree, Shrub & Palm Trimming in [Service Area]
- **Intro:** Overgrown palms and shrubs aren't just messy — they're a storm risk. Our
  licensed crew keeps everything trimmed, healthy, and hurricane-ready.

### 7d. Mulch, Rock & Beds · 7e. Sod Installation · 7f. Cleanups & Storm Debris

## 10. Contact / Free Estimate Form

The conversion endpoint — short, fast, mobile-first. Feeds the AI lead system
([proposal Section 10](proposal.md#10-lead-generation--ai-automation-opportunities)) so
every submission gets an instant auto-reply (the 21x lever) and lands in the CRM.

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Get Your Free Estimate                                   │
│  ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐ │
│  │ Name*                                               │ │
│  │ Phone*            Email                             │ │
│  │ Service needed ▾  (Maintenance / Design / Cleanup…) │ │
│  │ Property address / area ▾                           │ │
│  │ Tell us about your yard (optional)                  │ │
│  │ [ Upload a photo of your yard ] (optional)          │ │
│  │            [ Get My Free Estimate ]                 │ │
│  └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘ │
│  Prefer to talk?  📞 Call  ·  💬 Text  [phone]            │
│  "We reply fast — usually within minutes."                │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Form notes / best practices:**
- Keep required fields to **Name + Phone** (fewer fields = more submissions).
- **Photo upload** lets Ari pre-qualify and quote faster.
- On submit → instant text + email auto-reply ("Thanks [Name], we got it — we'll be in
  touch within the hour. — Ari Landscaping") → lead logged in CRM → owner notified.
- Sticky **click-to-call / click-to-text** on mobile so the highest-intent users never
  hunt for the number.
