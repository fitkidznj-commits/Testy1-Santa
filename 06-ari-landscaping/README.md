# Project 6 — Ari Landscaping Digital Growth Package

**Client:** Ari Landscaping — South Florida / Florida Keys *(service area: client to confirm)*
**Prepared by:** a.i. STaRR
**Goal:** A complete, turnkey online-presence package — website, Google Business
Profile, local SEO, social, video, and AI lead automation — that turns a great
landscaping crew with thin marketing into the most-found, most-booked landscaper in
its service area.

---

## Executive Summary

Ari Landscaping does the work that sells itself — clean lines, healthy plants, finished
yards homeowners are proud of. The problem is the one almost every home-service company
has: **the work is excellent, but the online presence is thin and inconsistent**, so
the phone rings less than the quality of the work deserves.

Landscaping is a *local-intent* business. When a homeowner wants a yard cleaned up, a
new bed designed, or weekly maintenance, they search "landscaper near me," they look at
the Google map pack, they read reviews, and they call two or three companies. The one
that **shows up, looks credible, and answers first** wins the job. Industry data is
blunt about this: responding to a new lead within 5 minutes increases conversion by up
to **21x** versus waiting an hour ([LeadSync](https://leadsync.me/blog/landscaping-lead-generation/)).

a.i. STaRR proposes a **Digital Growth Package**: a conversion-focused website with
dedicated service pages, a fully optimized Google Business Profile, a local-SEO and
review engine to win the map pack, a steady social presence built around before/after
proof, and an AI lead-capture-and-follow-up system that responds to every inquiry in
seconds and never lets a lead go cold.

**Headline outcome:** In 30 days, Ari Landscaping gets a live website + service pages,
an optimized Google Business Profile, a review engine, a 30-day content system posting
before/afters and seasonal tips, and an AI lead system that captures and follows up on
every quote request automatically.

**Recommended package:** **Growth ($3,000/mo)** + one-time website build — the right
fit for a single-location home-service business that needs both consistent content and
a real lead system. See [`proposal.md`](proposal.md#11-pricing-recommendations).
