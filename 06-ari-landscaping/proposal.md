## 4. Social Media Strategy (Facebook-led)

For a local landscaper, **Facebook is the primary social platform** — it's where
homeowners live, where neighborhood recommendations happen, and where before/after posts
get shared. Instagram runs as a secondary mirror for reels and younger/realtor audiences.

**Content pillars (the "5 P's"):**
1. **Proof (before/after)** — the highest-performing content; transformations sell.
2. **Plants & Place** — Keys/South Florida-specific tips (salt-tolerant natives, palm
   care, hurricane-smart planting) that show local expertise.
3. **Process & People** — crew at work, equipment, "how we do it," meet-the-team.
4. **Praise** — reviews, testimonials, repeat-client shout-outs.
5. **Promos & Seasonal** — free-estimate offers, maintenance plans, seasonal cleanups,
   storm prep.

**Cadence (Growth tier):** ~30 posts/month (≈1/day) across Facebook (primary) +
Instagram (mirror), 8–10 reels/month. Geotag the service area on every post; use local
hashtags (#[City]Landscaping, #FloridaKeysLandscaping) and a branded tag (#AriYardsDoneRight).

**Engagement plan:** Reply to every comment/DM fast (AI-assisted templates → route real
leads to the quote flow); ask happy clients to share/tag; run a monthly "ugliest yard /
biggest transformation" prompt to source UGC and engagement.

first place locals look and the engine behind the map pack

[LocalMighty](https://www.localmighty.com/blog/local-seo-for-landscapers/)).

| GBP element | a.i. STaRR action | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Claim & verify** | Claim/recover the profile; verify ownership | First step; *client to grant access* |
| **Primary category** | Set to core revenue driver (e.g., *Landscaper* or *Lawn care service*) | Matches Ari's main service — *client to confirm primary service* |
| **Secondary categories** | Add all true services (Landscape designer, Tree service, Irrigation, etc.) | Each category expands which searches Ari appears in |
| **Service area** | Define exact cities/zips Ari serves | *Client to confirm* — drives map-pack geography |
| **Services + descriptions** | List every service with keyword-rich descriptions | Mirrors website service pages (Section 8) |
| **Photos (15+)** | Upload real finished-project + before/after + crew photos | Google AI scores quality; **no stock, no blurry** — *client to provide* |
| **Hours & contact** | Accurate hours, phone, booking link to the quote form | Powers click-to-call + directions |
| **GBP Posts (weekly)** | Auto-publish weekly posts: offers, before/afters, seasonal tips | Keeps the profile active = ranking signal |
| **Q&A** | Seed common questions (free estimates? service area? licensed?) | Pre-answers buyer objections |
| **Review engine** | Automated post-job review requests + owner responses | See Section 6 + Section 10 |

**Why it matters:** an active, photo-rich, fully-categorized GBP with steady reviews is
the difference between page-one map pack and invisibility. Most local competitors leave
this half-built — Ari can own it.

## 7. Content Strategy

Content does double duty: it **ranks** (local SEO) and it **converts** (proof + trust).
Two streams:

**A. Social content** — the 5 P's pillars (Section 4), executed in the
[30-day content plan](30-day-content-plan.md): daily before/afters, seasonal tips,
crew/process, reviews, and offers.

**B. Website / SEO content** — Q&A-format pages and tips that answer real homeowner
questions and target local keywords ([LocalMighty](https://www.localmighty.com/blog/local-seo-for-landscapers/)):

| Content piece | Targets | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Service pages (Section 8) | "[service] [city]" | Core ranking + conversion |
| "Best salt-tolerant plants for [area] yards" | Local + informational intent | Ranks; shows local expertise |
| "How to prep your landscape for hurricane season in the Keys" | Seasonal local intent | Timely, highly shareable |
| "Palm trimming: how often and why it matters in South Florida" | Service-adjacent | Drives a specific service |
| "What does landscaping cost in [area]?" (ranges + free estimate CTA) | High commercial intent | Pre-qualifies + captures leads |
| FAQ page | "is [Ari] licensed/insured," "do you offer free estimates" | Answers objections, feeds GBP Q&A |

*The before/after library is the engine for both streams — client to provide photos so
we can produce at volume.*

## 8. Service-Page Recommendations

Each service gets its **own dedicated, SEO-optimized page** (not one lumped "Services"
page) so Ari can rank for specific high-intent searches. Recommended pages — *client to
confirm which services apply*:

| Service page | Target search | Recommended copy outline |

| **Landscape Design & Installation** | "landscape design [city]" | Hook → design process → plant selection (salt-tolerant natives) → install gallery → CTA |
| **Mulch, Rock & Beds** | "mulch installation [city]" | Hook → bed refresh value → materials → photos → CTA |
| **Trimming & Pruning (incl. palms)** | "palm trimming [city]" | Hook → why/when in South FL → safety/licensed → before/after → CTA |
| **Sod Installation** | "sod installation [city]" | Hook → sod types for FL → prep + install steps → warranty → CTA |
| **Irrigation / Sprinklers** *(if offered)* | "sprinkler repair [city]" | Hook → repair vs. install → water-saving → CTA |

**Every service page includes:** a clear H1 with service + area, a benefit-led intro, a

## 9. Video Strategy (Summary)

Short-form video amplifies the same proof that wins jobs. For a landscaper, the highest-
performing formats are:

- **Before/after transformation reels** — split-screen or wipe transition; the
  single best-converting video format for landscaping.
- **Time-lapse of a job** — overgrown to manicured in 15 seconds.
- **"Process" reels** — sod going down, beds getting edged, a palm getting trimmed.
- **Tip reels** — "3 salt-tolerant plants that survive Keys wind" (local expertise).
- **Meet-the-crew / day-in-the-life** — builds trust and personality.

**AI acceleration (a.i. STaRR stack):** a Creatomate template turns a shared photo/clip
library + captions into branded before/after reels on autopilot; HeyGen produces
optional voiceover/presenter tip videos without a shoot. **One premium brand video
(45–60s)** — "South Florida yards, done right" — anchors the website hero and ads.

Detailed reel storyboards are available as a production add-on; the social reels are
scheduled within the [30-day content plan](30-day-content-plan.md).

## 10. Lead Generation & AI Automation Opportunities

The strategy that wins landscaping: **show up (SEO/GBP) → capture every lead → respond
in seconds → follow up until booked.** Using the standard a.i. STaRR stack
([brand guide](../_shared/brand-guide.md#5-standard-ai-automation-stack)):

| Workflow | What it does | Tools | Hours saved/wk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Instant lead responder** | Quote-form/DM/missed-call → auto text+email reply in seconds (the 21x lever) | Make.com + SMS/email + Meta API | ~3 |
| **Lead capture + CRM** | Every inquiry logged to Google Sheets CRM with source, service, status | Make.com + Google Sheets | ~3 |
| **Follow-up sequence** | If no reply, automated 3-touch follow-up (text/email) over a few days | Make.com + SMS/email | ~3 |
| **Review engine** | After job completion, timed review request with one-tap Google link; routes 5-star public, flags <4-star privately | Make.com + SMS/email | ~2 |
| **Content engine** | Generates monthly calendar + draft posts/GBP posts from pillars; routes to approval | ChatGPT/Claude + Sheets | ~4 |
| **Auto-posting** | Publishes approved posts/reels + weekly GBP posts on schedule | Make.com + Meta + GBP API | ~3 |
| **Reporting dashboard** | Leads, sources, response time, reviews, rankings, top posts — one live link | Make.com + Looker Studio/Sheets | ~1 |

**Lead-gen recommendations (priority order):**
1. **Turn on the instant responder first** — it's the highest-ROI lever (21x).
2. **Optimize GBP + reviews** — the highest-intent free traffic source.
3. **Launch the website + service pages** — converts that traffic into quote requests.
4. **Run the follow-up sequence** — no lead goes cold; peak-season overflow gets caught.
5. **(Optional, peak season)** Google Local Service Ads — 50–70% lead-to-customer rates
   ([LeadSync](https://leadsync.me/blog/landscaping-lead-generation/)); add via the paid
   add-on once organic is humming.

**Net effect:** ~15+ hours/week of manual marketing and lead chasing removed, every
lead answered in seconds, and a steady review + content rhythm that compounds rankings.
