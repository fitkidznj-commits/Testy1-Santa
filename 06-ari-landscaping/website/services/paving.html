Paving FAQ

How long does a paver driveway last in the Keys?

With proper base prep and occasional sealing, quality pavers last decades — and individual pavers can be lifted and replaced if ever needed, unlike poured concrete.

Pavers or concrete — which is better here?

Pavers handle our heat, salt, and ground movement well, resist cracking, and are easy to repair. We'll walk you through options for your project and budget.

Do you handle drainage and grading?

Yes. Proper grading and drainage are part of every install so water moves away from your home and your hardscape lasts.

Can you seal or repair my existing pavers?

Absolutely — we re-level, re-sand, seal, and repair existing paver surfaces to bring them back to life.
