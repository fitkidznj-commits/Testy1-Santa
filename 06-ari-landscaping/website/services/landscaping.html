Landscaping FAQ

How often should I mow in the Florida Keys?

In our climate, weekly mowing in the growing season keeps turf healthiest; biweekly works in cooler months. We'll recommend a schedule during your free estimate.

Do you offer one-time cuts and cleanups?

Yes — one-time cleanups, storm debris removal, and single visits are all available alongside recurring maintenance.

What plants survive best in the Keys?

Salt- and wind-tolerant natives such as gumbo-limbo, seagrape, and sabal palm. We design around what thrives here so your landscape lasts.
