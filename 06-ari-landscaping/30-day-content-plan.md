## Sample Graphic Specs (art direction for Canva)

> a.i. STaRR renders these on the Ari brand kit as a production add-on. Recommended kit:
> palm green `#2E5E3A`, leaf green `#6FAE5B`, sand `#E8DDC7`, charcoal `#222`, white.
> *(Final palette tuned to Ari's logo — client to provide.)*

1. **Before/After template** — vertical 1080×1350. Split layout with a center divider
   and "BEFORE / AFTER" labels (sand banner, green text). Ari logo top-left, ⭐ rating
   top-right, CTA bar bottom: "Free Estimate — [phone]." Reusable for every transformation.

2. **Review screenshot card** — 1080×1080. Real Google review screenshot inset on a
   palm-green background, 5 gold stars, large pull-quote, "— [Name], [City]," logo +
   "Get yours done right." Built once, swapped per review.

3. **Free-estimate offer** — 1080×1080. Big bold "FREE ESTIMATE" headline on leaf green,
   sand sub-bar "Same day · No pressure," lawn/yard photo strip, click-to-call phone +
   QR/link prompt, logo.

4. **Plant / local-tip card** — 1080×1350. Hero plant photo (e.g., gumbo-limbo), "Salt-
   tolerant. Wind-tough. Keys-proof." headline, 2–3 bullet benefits, "Save this 🔖" prompt,
   logo. Establishes local expertise; great for shares/saves.

5. **Maintenance-plan / referral promo** — 1080×1080. Clean green-and-sand layout,
   "Set it & forget it" or "Refer a neighbor" headline, simple 3-step or offer detail,
   DM/CTA bar, logo. Swap copy for promo vs. referral.
