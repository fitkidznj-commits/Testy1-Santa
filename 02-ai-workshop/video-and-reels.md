## Video Strategy (recap)

- **One 60-second marketing video** anchors the campaign — the host, to camera, on the
  pain → the promise → the invite. Used on the [landing page](copy-and-sequences.md#2-registration-landing-page-copy),
  the [Facebook Event](copy-and-sequences.md#8-facebook-event-strategy) cover, and as
  the **personal video in the confirmation email** (research's most underused show-up
  tactic).
- **5+ short promo reels** carry the daily organic promotion across the
  [3-week window](proposal.md#4-social-media-strategy).
- **AI acceleration:** **HeyGen** produces a polished host/presenter video and reminder
  clips without a full shoot; **Creatomate** templates render the countdown and "what
  you'll learn" reels at scale; **Canva** (a.i. STaRR brand kit) produces the graphic
  set.

## Marketing Video Script

**"Let AI Do Your Marketing" — 60s host-to-camera invite.** Tone: warm, confident,
local. Setting: a recognizable Keys spot (dock, waterfront cafe, or marina) so it reads
as "one of us." End on a clear RSVP CTA.

| Time | Shot | Voiceover (host, to camera) | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:05 | Host walks toward camera, Keys waterfront behind | "If you run a business in the Keys, I bet marketing is the thing you do at 11pm — if you do it at all." | **Spending nights on marketing?** |
| 0:05–0:15 | Quick cuts: owner on phone late, empty social feed, sticky-note to-dos | "Posting every day, getting found on Google, following up with leads — there's never time." | "No time. No system. Sound familiar?" |
| 0:15–0:28 | Screen-record overlay: AI writing captions fast; calendar filling | "Here's the good news: AI can do most of it for you. A week of posts in ten minutes. Reviews and follow-up on autopilot." | **AI can run it for you.** |
| 0:28–0:42 | Back to host; warm, direct | "I'm hosting a **free** 90-minute workshop for Keys business owners. No tech background needed. Every example is a real Keys business — and you'll walk out with tools you can use this week." | "FREE Workshop · 90 min · Made for the Keys" |
| 0:42–0:52 | Lower-third with event details; b-roll of a friendly room/audience | "Get found. Get followed. Get booked — without spending your nights on it." | **[Date] · [Time] · [Venue]** |
| 0:52–0:60 | Host smiles; logo end card | "Seats are limited — save yours free at the link. See you there." | **Save your seat — free · [link]**  ·  a.i. STaRR logo |

*Production options: shoot in ~1 hour at a Keys location → a.i. STaRR edits the 60s
cut + pulls the promo reels below from the same footage. **HeyGen** can produce a fully
AI-presenter version (and the confirmation-email + reminder clips) with no shoot.*

## Graphic Specs (art direction for Canva)

> a.i. STaRR renders these on the a.i. STaRR brand kit in Canva as a production add-on.
> **Brand kit (matches the [Whale Harbor](../01-whale-harbor/30-day-content-plan.md)
> palette):** ocean navy `#0B3C5D`, surf teal `#1B7A8C`, sand `#E8D9B5`, sunset coral
> `#E8743B`, white. Clean sans headlines; warm script accents used sparingly.

1. **Main event flyer** — 1080×1350. Bold headline **"AI for Your Keys Business"**
   top; subhead "Get Found. Get Followed. Get Booked."; **FREE** badge in sunset coral;
   date/time/venue block; "Seats limited" line; QR code + "[link]"; a.i. STaRR logo.
   Ocean-navy background, sand + teal accents, a friendly host photo or a simple AI/
   robot-meets-palm motif. Shareable into [groups](proposal.md#5-group-promotion-strategy).

2. **"What you'll learn" carousel** — 1080×1080, 5 slides. Slide 1 title; slides 2–5 one
   takeaway each (Posts in 10 min · Get found on Google · Reviews on autopilot · Never
   miss a lead), big icon + one line each; slide 5 CTA "Save your seat — free." Navy/
   teal gradient, coral CTA buttons.

3. **Countdown / "Seats filling" graphic** — 1080×1080 (template, re-render per beat).
   Huge number ("3 DAYS LEFT" / "ALMOST FULL"), event details strip, coral CTA bar
   "RSVP free — [link]." Used 1 week / 3 days / day-of and as a reminder visual.

4. **Host intro / credibility card** — 1080×1350. Host photo, name + "a.i. STaRR," one
   line of local proof, tagline "Local soul. AI speed.," and "Hosting a free workshop —
   [date]." Warm, trust-building; sand background, navy text.

5. **Chamber / partner co-brand blurb card** — 1080×1080. Space for a partner/Chamber
   logo beside the a.i. STaRR logo, the [short event blurb](copy-and-sequences.md#3-event-description),
   and RSVP link — a one-tap-shareable asset for [referral partners and Chambers](proposal.md#5-group-promotion-strategy).

6. **"Thanks for coming" / recap card** — 1080×1080. Used after the event for the
   [Facebook Event](copy-and-sequences.md#8-facebook-event-strategy) recap and as proof
   for the next workshop. Photo of the room, "Thanks, Keys! 🙌," and a soft "Next session
   coming — get on the list" CTA.
