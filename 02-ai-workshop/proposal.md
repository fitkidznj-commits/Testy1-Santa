a.i. STaRR is running a live workshop — **"AI for Your Keys Business"** — that teaches

## 2. Research Findings

### The workshop opportunity (AI training is in demand)
- AI training for small businesses has gone mainstream: universities, the U.S. Chamber,
  Google's "Grow with Google" program, and Small Business Development Centers (SBDCs)
  are all rolling out AI-for-small-business curricula in 2025–2026. Demand exists; what
  local owners lack is a **practical, local, hands-on** session run by someone who
  knows their market.
- The pattern that works: **demystify AI, keep it hands-on, use real-world use cases**
  so owners leave with confidence and something they can use Monday morning — not a
  lecture on the theory of large language models.
- **a.i. STaRR's edge:** national programs are generic and remote. We teach AI through
  the lens of a Keys restaurant, marina, vacation rental, or home-services business —
  with examples from this market and a local face in the room.

[Grow with Google – AI for Small Businesses](https://grow.google/ai-for-small-businesses/),

### The Florida Keys market (who's in the room)
- Monroe County has **just over 80,000 residents** and a labor force around **50,000**.
  Tourism is the dominant engine — visitors spend roughly **$3.5 billion annually**,
  supporting **24,000+ jobs**, and recreation/tourism accounts for **33%–75% of the
  local economy** depending on how it's measured.
- That means the addressable audience is overwhelmingly **tourism-adjacent small
  businesses**: restaurants, bars, marinas/charters, vacation rentals, watersports,
  retail/boutiques, and the home-services businesses that support them — exactly
  a.i. STaRR's [target clients](../_shared/brand-guide.md#6-target-clients-florida-keys).
- The market is **geographically linear and tight-knit** (Key Largo → Marathon along
  one highway) and **relationship-driven**. Chambers, town Facebook groups, and a
  handful of well-connected referral partners reach most owners. This is why an
  **organic, community-first** strategy can fill a room where cold paid ads would burn
  budget on a thin, spread-out population.
- Active local institutions to leverage: the **Islamorada Chamber of Commerce**, the
  **Key Largo Chamber**, the **Greater Marathon Chamber**, and existing community/
  marketing Facebook groups (e.g., the Tavernier-based "Florida Keys Marketing Group").
  *Specific group names, member counts, and admin contacts — client to confirm.*

### Registration & attendance benchmarks (what fills a room)
- Across industries, average live attendance is **~56%** of registrants — meaning **4 in
  10 people who register never show up.** A strong reminder sequence is the single
  biggest lever on actual butts-in-seats.
- A **3-touch email reminder sequence** (confirmation → ~24h before → ~1h before) lifts
  show-up from roughly **40% to 55–65%.**
- **Adding SMS reminders lifts attendance another ~12–20%.** A combined personalized
  email + 3-touch SMS sequence has pushed registrant-to-attendee conversion from ~56%
  to as high as **71%** — a ~27% lift over baseline.
- Most underused tactic: a **short personal video** from the host in the confirmation
  email — it creates emotional investment before the event.
- Implication for a free, in-person, local workshop: expect a **higher no-show
  temptation** than a paid event, so over-invest in reminders, calendar holds, and SMS,
  and **overbook the room** by a planned margin. *Exact room capacity and overbooking
  ratio — client to confirm.*

*Sources:* [ClickMeeting – Webinars That Convert (2025)](https://blog.clickmeeting.com/webinars-that-convert),
[WebinarNinja – Reminder Emails](https://webinarninja.com/blog/webinar-reminder-email/),
[AmraAndElma – Webinar Funnel Statistics 2025](https://www.amraandelma.com/webinar-funnel-statistics/),
[Mobile Text Alerts – SMS for Webinar Reminders](https://mobile-text-alerts.com/articles/top-sms-solutions-webinar-registration-reminders).

## 3. Marketing Strategy

**Positioning:** *"The AI marketing class for Keys business owners — taught by someone
who actually works this market."* Not a tech lecture. A practical, local, leave-with-
something workshop that helps you get found, get followed, and get booked with less time
spent on marketing.

**Core promise (mirrors the brand [engagement promise](../_shared/brand-guide.md#7-engagement-promise)):**
"In 90 minutes you'll walk out with AI tools and a simple system you can use this week —
plus a clear picture of what marketing-on-autopilot could look like for your business."

**Target audiences & key messages:**
- **Restaurants / bars / cafes** → "Post every day without spending your night on it."
- **Marinas / charters / watersports** → "Turn one good catch photo into a week of content and more bookings."
- **Vacation rentals / hospitality** → "Fill more nights with reviews and content that run themselves."
- **Home services (paint/remodel/landscaping)** → "Get found on Google and follow up with every lead automatically."
- **Retail / boutiques** → "Show up daily on social without hiring a marketing person."

**Channels (all organic):** Town & Keys-wide **Facebook groups**, the three **Chambers
of Commerce**, **referral partners** (POS reps, accountants, the Chamber, suppliers),
a.i. STaRR's own **Instagram/Facebook**, **email/SMS** to existing contacts, a
**Facebook Event**, and **word-of-mouth flyers/QR** at high-traffic local spots
(coffee shops, marinas, co-working). No paid ads.

**The 90-day arc:**
- **Days 1–14 — Build & seed:** finalize date/venue, stand up the [landing page](copy-and-sequences.md#2-registration-landing-page-copy)
  and [Facebook Event](copy-and-sequences.md#8-facebook-event-strategy), brief referral
  partners and Chambers, prep the [automation](#10-ai-automation-opportunities).
- **Days 15–30 — Promote & fill:** run the [group promotion calendar](#5-group-promotion-strategy)
  and the [3-email invite sequence](copy-and-sequences.md#4-email-sequence-3-touch);
  daily organic social; reminders begin as people register.
- **Day ~30–35 — Run the workshop:** SMS/email reminders peak; deliver the session;
  capture leads and book consults from the room.
- **Days 35–90 — Convert & repeat:** run the [follow-up sequence](copy-and-sequences.md#7-follow-up-sequence-attendees--no-shows),
  book discovery calls, close [Growth-tier retainers](../_shared/pricing-framework.md#1-core-monthly-packages),
  and template the whole system to run the workshop again in the next town.

## 4. Social Media Strategy

**Platforms:** Facebook (primary — this is where Keys community/business groups live),
Instagram (secondary — reels + countdown), email/SMS (owned list).

**Content pillars (the "4 W's"):**
1. **Why (the pain)** — "Spending nights on marketing? There's a better way." Relatable
   owner pain points.
2. **Wins (proof)** — quick AI demos, before/after, "I made a week of posts in 10
   minutes" clips.
3. **What you'll learn (agenda)** — bite-size previews of each workshop takeaway.
4. **Who & where (logistics + FOMO)** — host bio, "seats filling," countdown, location.

**Cadence (promo window, ~3 weeks out):**

| Window | Facebook (groups + page) | Instagram | Email/SMS |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 weeks out | Announcement post + Event created | Announcement reel | Invite email #1 |
| 2 weeks out | 2× value posts (tip + demo clip) | 2× reels + countdown sticker | — |
| 1 week out | "Seats filling" + testimonial-style post | "What you'll learn" carousel | Nurture email #2 |
| 3 days out | Last-call post in each group | Last-call reel | Last-call email #3 |
| Day of | "Doors open tonight" + live stories | Day-of stories/countdown | SMS reminders (per [sequence](copy-and-sequences.md#6-reminder-sequence-email--sms)) |

**Engagement plan:** reply to every comment and DM within hours (AI-assisted templates);
ask registrants to "tag a business owner who needs this"; reshare any partner/Chamber
posts; pin the Event to the a.i. STaRR page. **Geo/tags:** Islamorada, Key Largo,
Tavernier, Marathon; **#KeysBusiness #FloridaKeys #AIforBusiness #ShopLocalKeys.**

Full reel concepts, the marketing-video script, and graphic specs are in

## 5. Group Promotion Strategy

The organic engine. The Keys is reachable through a small set of **town Facebook
groups, Chambers, and Keys-wide pages** — coordinated outreach across these fills the
room. **Golden rule:** every group has its own culture and rules. **Lead with value
(a free local class), never spam.** DM admins first, post natively (no naked links
where banned — use "comment 'AI' and I'll send the link" or the Event), and respect each
group's promo-day rules.

> *All group names, membership sizes, admin contacts, and posting rules are
> **client to confirm** — verify current rules with each group admin before posting.*

| Town / Group | Where / how to reach owners | What to post | Cadence | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Islamorada** | Islamorada community + business FB groups; **Islamorada Chamber** (newsletter, member email, FB page); central venue means home-base promotion | Announcement → 1 value post → last-call; ask Chamber to share to members | 3 wks / 1 wk / 3 days | Strongest pull if venue is here; partner with Chamber for credibility |
| **Key Largo** | Key Largo community FB groups; **Key Largo Chamber**; "Shop/Eat Key Largo" type pages | Same 3-touch; emphasize "short drive, worth it" + replay option | 3 wks / 1 wk / 3 days | Northernmost — offer replay to reduce drive friction |
| **Tavernier** | Tavernier neighborhood groups; the **Florida Keys Marketing Group** (Tavernier-based) is an ideal partner | Co-brand the post with the marketing group if possible; value-first | 3 wks / 1 wk / 3 days | Marketing-minded audience — high relevance, treat as a partner not just a channel |
| **Marathon** | Greater Marathon community + business FB groups; **Greater Marathon Chamber** | Same 3-touch; push the **live-stream/replay** hard (longest drive) | 3 wks / 1 wk / 3 days | Strongly promote virtual option; consider a future Marathon-hosted session |
| **Florida Keys–wide groups** | Keys-wide business/community + "buy/sell/recommend" groups; Keys-wide marketing pages | Broad announcement + Event link; "open to all Keys owners" | 3 wks / 1 wk / 3 days | Honor each group's promo-day rules; native post + Event, not bare links |
| **Chambers (all 3)** | Member emails, newsletters, event calendars, FB pages | Provide ready-to-share blurb + graphic + Event link | Once at launch + 1 reminder | Offer to co-host / give Chamber members priority seating |
| **Referral partners** | POS/booking reps, accountants, suppliers, prior contacts | Personal DM/email + forwardable blurb | 1 ask + 1 nudge | Warmest channel — give them a forward-ready [invite](copy-and-sequences.md#4-email-sequence-3-touch) |

**Execution tips:**
- **DM the admin first** for each group, introduce the free workshop, and ask the best
  way to share. Admin goodwill > guessing the rules.
- **Native posts beat links.** Where links are restricted, post the value + "RSVP on our
  Facebook Event (linked in comments)" or a comment-to-get-link prompt.
- **One forward-ready blurb** (see [event description](copy-and-sequences.md#3-event-description))
  + one graphic so partners and Chambers can share in one click.
- **Track source per registrant** (landing-page field "How did you hear about us?") so
  we learn which groups/towns actually fill seats and double down next round.

## 10. AI Automation Opportunities

Using the standard a.i. STaRR stack (see [brand guide](../_shared/brand-guide.md#5-standard-ai-automation-stack)).
This automation is the workshop's engine **and** a live demo of what a.i. STaRR sells —
the room watches their own confirmation/reminder texts arrive.

| Workflow | What it does | Tools | Hours saved/wk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Registration capture** | Landing-page/Event RSVP → row in Google Sheets (name, email, phone, business, source) → instant confirmation email (with host video) + welcome SMS + calendar invite | Make.com + Sheets + email/SMS | ~3 |
| **Promo auto-posting** | Schedules the promo posts/reels to FB page + IG from the content calendar; routes group-post reminders to the team | Make.com + Meta API + Sheets | ~3 |
| **Reminder sequence** | Triggers the timed [email + SMS reminders](copy-and-sequences.md#6-reminder-sequence-email--sms) (1 wk / 1 day / 2 hrs before) off each registrant's row | Make.com + email/SMS | ~2 |
| **Attendance + no-show tagging** | Marks each registrant attended/no-show at check-in (form or tap) → branches them into the right follow-up | Make.com + Sheets | ~1 |
| **Follow-up sequence** | Sends the [attendee](copy-and-sequences.md#7-follow-up-sequence-attendees--no-shows) (resources + book-a-call) and [no-show](copy-and-sequences.md#7-follow-up-sequence-attendees--no-shows) (replay + reschedule) branches automatically | Make.com + email/SMS | ~2 |
| **Lead → consult booking** | "Book a free strategy call" interest → calendar link + reminder + CRM tag for the sales follow-up | Make.com + Sheets + email/SMS | ~2 |
| **Reporting** | Live dashboard: registrations by source/town, show-up rate, consults booked, clients closed | Make.com + Sheets/Looker Studio | ~1 |

**Registration → reminder → follow-up flow (the spine):**
RSVP form → **Google Sheets** master list (source-tagged) → **instant confirmation**
(email w/ host video + calendar hold + welcome SMS) → **reminder branch** fires at
**1 week / 1 day / 2 hours** before (email + SMS) → at the event, **check-in tags**
attended vs. no-show → **follow-up branch:** attendees get resources + a book-a-call
link; no-shows get the replay + a reschedule offer → interested leads route to a
**consult booking** + sales CRM tag → everything reports to a **live dashboard.**

**Net effect:** ~14+ hours/week of manual event-marketing work removed, a measurably
higher show-up rate from the SMS+email reminders, and a follow-up engine that keeps
working long after the room empties — while the whole thing demos the agency's product.
