# Project 2 — AI Workshop Marketing System

**Client / Initiative:** a.i. STaRR — "AI for Your Keys Business" Workshop
**Audience:** Florida Keys small-business owners (Key Largo → Marathon)
**Prepared by:** a.i. STaRR
**Goal:** Fill every seat at a live AI workshop for Keys business owners — **organically,
with no paid ads** — using local community channels, a registration funnel, and an
automated reminder/follow-up system that doubles as a demo of what a.i. STaRR sells.

---

## Executive Summary

a.i. STaRR is hosting a live workshop — **"AI for Your Keys Business: Get Found, Get
Followed, Get Booked"** — that teaches local owners how to use AI to run their
marketing in less time. The workshop is both a service to the community and the top of
a.i. STaRR's own sales funnel: the room is full of exactly our [target clients](../_shared/brand-guide.md#6-target-clients-florida-keys).

The challenge is filling seats **without paid ads**. The Keys is a tight, relationship-
driven, ~80,000-resident market where tourism touches a third to three-quarters of the
local economy and word-of-mouth moves faster than any algorithm. That is a strength: a
small number of the right community groups, chambers, and referral partners can fill a
room organically — if the outreach is coordinated, the registration page converts, and
the reminder system actually gets people through the door (4 in 10 registrants normally
no-show; a strong email + SMS sequence closes most of that gap).

This project delivers the complete marketing system to do it: a town-by-town **group
promotion strategy**, a high-converting **registration landing page**, a 3-email
**invite/nurture/last-call sequence**, an email + **SMS reminder sequence**, an
**attendee/no-show follow-up sequence**, a **Facebook Event** playbook, and the
**Make.com automation** that runs registration → reminders → follow-up on autopilot.

**Headline outcome:** A repeatable, ad-free workshop-marketing system that fills seats
from local community channels, converts attendees into a.i. STaRR clients, and proves
the agency's own product live in front of the room.

**Recommended engagement:** Event/launch campaign build, **$3,500–$7,500** one-time
(see [`proposal.md`](proposal.md#11-pricing-recommendations)), with the workshop funnel
designed to convert attendees onto the [Growth retainer](../_shared/pricing-framework.md#1-core-monthly-packages).
