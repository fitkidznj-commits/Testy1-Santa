## 8. Facebook Event Strategy

Facebook is where the Keys business community lives, so the **Facebook Event** is the
organic hub everything points to. It creates social proof ("X going / Y interested"),
sends FB's own reminders for free, and is shareable into the
[town groups](proposal.md#5-group-promotion-strategy).

**Setup**
- Create from the **a.i. STaRR Facebook Page** (not a personal profile) so it's
  brandable and reusable.
- Use the [long event description](#3-event-description), the
  [marketing video](video-and-reels.md#marketing-video-script) as the cover, accurate
  date/time/location, and a **registration link** in the Event details (so we capture
  contact info for SMS — don't rely on FB RSVPs alone).
- Co-host with the **Chamber(s) or a referral partner** where possible — it exposes the
  Event to their followers organically.

**Promotion cadence (organic)**
| When | Action |
|---|---|
| 3 weeks out | Publish Event; share to a.i. STaRR page; post + share into town/Keys groups (per [group strategy](proposal.md#5-group-promotion-strategy)); invite relevant connections |
| 2 weeks out | Post a **value clip/discussion** in the Event (a quick AI tip) to keep it active in feeds |
| 1 week out | "One week left — [X] going" post; ask interested attendees to "invite a business owner who needs this" |
| 3 days out | Last-call post in the Event + reshare to groups |
| Day of | "Doors open today" post + live stories; thank attendees in the Event afterward |

**Engagement rules**
- Reply to **every** comment/question in the Event within hours (AI-assisted templates).
- Encourage shares and tags — each share is free reach into a new owner's network.
- Post a recap + photos afterward so the Event becomes proof for the **next** workshop.

> **Note:** "Interested/Going" on Facebook is soft intent — always drive people to the
> [landing page](#2-registration-landing-page-copy) so we capture email + mobile for the
> [reminder sequence](#5-reminder-sequence-email--sms). FB reminders alone won't hit the
> show-up rates the SMS+email sequence does.
