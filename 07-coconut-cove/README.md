## Executive Summary

Coconut Cove is a **7-acre old-style Florida Keys resort and marina** in the heart of
Islamorada — family-owned by Paul Bates since 1994. It quietly does something few Keys
properties can: it stacks an **oceanfront private-estate wedding venue** (Tiki bar,
lighted dance floor, sunset ceremonies, fireworks) on top of a **boutique lodging
resort** (20 rooms, Captain's cottages, General's rooms), a **working marina**
(10 slips, watersports, dive & fishing charters), a **zero-entry waterfall pool**,
and a **private beach** — all on one tropical estate that doubles as a film/production
location.

The opportunity: that three-in-one story — *getaway, wedding, and marina* — is rarely
told as one coordinated narrative across social. a.i. STaRR proposes a unified content
system that markets Coconut Cove as **the** intimate, all-in-one Islamorada escape:
daily posting, reels that sell the sunset-wedding-and-waterfront vibe, a wedding-lead
funnel, a review engine, and AI automation that keeps it all running.

**Headline outcome:** A complete 30-day content system live in 30 days, daily posting
across Instagram/Facebook, a wedding-and-getaway lead engine, a review-generation
engine, and a live performance dashboard — positioning Coconut Cove as the most-booked
boutique resort-and-wedding venue in the Upper Keys.

**Recommended package:** Growth ($3,000/mo) — boutique scale with a high-value wedding
funnel fits Growth, with a clear path to Premium in peak season. See
[`proposal.md`](proposal.md#11-pricing-recommendations).
