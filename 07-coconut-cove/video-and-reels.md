## Video Strategy

- **Short-form reels carry the daily vibe** — waterfall pool, sunset ceremonies,
  fireworks, marina, cottage life. High volume, trend-driven, shot on phone + drone.
- **Premium videos anchor the brand** — evergreen hero films used on the website, OTAs,
  the wedding directories (WeddingWire/The Knot/Pinterest), ads, and pinned social.
- **The wedding film is the money asset** — a single great wedding film is the highest-
  ROI piece of content this resort can own; it works for years across every channel.
- **AI acceleration:** a templated reel system in Creatomate turns a shared shot library
  + captions into branded reels on autopilot; HeyGen produces voiceover/presenter
  segments for promos, package announcements, and recaps without a shoot.

## Premium Video Concepts

### 1. "Your Own Piece of the Keys" — Hero Brand Film (90s) — *tourism/getaway*
The flagship. Follows a couple/family through a full cove day: cottage morning coffee at
the picnic table, waterfall pool, private beach, a charter or kayak on the water, Tiki
golden hour, fireworks at night. Cinematic drone + handheld. Light VO ties it together.
Used on the homepage, OTAs, ads, and pinned post.

### 2. "Ends with Fireworks" — Wedding Film (60s) — *wedding*
The money asset. A real sunset ceremony on the beach, the whole-estate reception, the
lighted Tiki dance floor, and the fireworks finale. Romantic, golden-toned, emotional.
Drives the wedding-inquiry funnel and feeds Pinterest/WeddingWire. Inquiry CTA.

### 3. "The Quiet Keys Getaway" — Vacation Film (45s) — *vacation*
Sells the boutique, owner-run, no-crowds alternative to the big resorts: waterfall pool,
private beach, cottage life, walkable Islamorada. Targets families and couples who want
calm over party. Direct-booking CTA.

### 4. "Marina Home Base" — Watersports Film (45s) — *marina/vacation*
Boats at the seawall, sunrise off the bow, waverunners and kayaks, dive/fishing charter
out and back, the dock-to-Tiki handoff. Sells slips + watersports to boaters and anglers.
