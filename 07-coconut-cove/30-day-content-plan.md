## Proposed Coconut Cove Brand Kit (for client sign-off)

> No published brand kit was located in research, so a.i. STaRR proposes the following,
> tuned to the resort's tropical / old-Keys identity. Final palette to be confirmed
> against the resort's logo and signage.

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Cove teal | `#0E7C7B` | Primary brand color, headlines, frames |
| Lagoon aqua | `#3FB8AF` | Accents, buttons, pool/water content |
| Sand | `#EFE3C8` | Backgrounds, cards |
| Coral sunset | `#F0653A` | CTAs, highlights, sunset content |
| Palm green | `#2E6B3E` | Secondary accent, nature/marina |
| Deep navy | `#0B2E3C` | Text, overlays, night/fireworks content |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Logo, type on photos |

**Type:** clean humanist sans for headlines/UI (e.g. Poppins/Montserrat); a warm script
used *sparingly* for wedding content (e.g. for "I do" / couple names). **Logo:** keep the
"Coconut Cove Resort & Marina" master lockup consistent across IG, FB, OTA, and the
wedding directories.

See the [video & reels companion](video-and-reels.md) for moving content and the
[proposal](proposal.md#4-social-media-strategy) for the pillar strategy.
