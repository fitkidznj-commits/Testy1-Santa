## 1. Executive Summary

See [`README.md`](README.md#executive-summary) for the one-page summary. In short:
Coconut Cove is a family-owned, 7-acre old-style Keys resort that bundles three
revenue stories most properties have to choose between — a **boutique getaway**
(20 rooms, waterfall pool, private beach), a **private-estate wedding venue** (Tiki
bar, sunset ceremonies, fireworks), and a **working marina** (slips, watersports,
charters). a.i. STaRR will turn that into one daily-told story across social, backed by
a wedding-lead funnel, a review engine, and AI automation — positioning Coconut Cove as
the Upper Keys' most-booked boutique resort-and-wedding escape.

## 2. Research Findings

### The property (client to confirm specifics)
- **Location:** 84801 Overseas Hwy, Islamorada, FL — Windley Key, ~MM 84.8, roughly 70
  miles south of Miami in the center of the resort strip.
- **Ownership / heritage:** Family-owned and operated by **Paul Bates since 1994** —
  an "old-style Florida Keys" resort on a **7-acre tropical estate**. This independent,
  owner-run heritage is a genuine brand asset.
- **Lodging:** ~**20 air-conditioned rooms**.
  - **Captain's cottages** — lowest-priced rooms; full kitchen and a picnic table right
    out front. Casual, value, families.
  - **General's rooms** — in the more modern building; larger groups and wedding parties.
  - Rooms noted with sea views, balconies, kitchenettes.
- **Pool & beach:** Large **zero-entry pool with a waterfall**, shaded by palms;
  **private beach**, oceanfront lounging, seawall, Jacuzzi.
- **Marina:** Private marina, **~10 slips, boats up to 30 ft**. Within walking distance:
  boat, kayak, and waverunner rentals; fishing guides; dive charters and instruction.
- **Dining / events:** On-site **Tiki bar and oceanfront restaurant**, **lighted Tiki
  dance floor**, covered open venue, BBQ grills, free-weight gym, free breakfast and
  parking, complimentary Wi-Fi (client to confirm current F&B operating status).
- **Weddings & production:** Specializes in **world-class private-estate weddings** —
  full planning, all-night covered venue, **sunset ceremonies with an evening fireworks
  display**. Also hosts **films, commercials, and still-photo production** with
  production-assistant services.
- **Nearby draws:** Theater of the Sea, Windley Key Fossil Reef Geological State Park,
  and the Islamorada restaurant/bar strip — all within walking distance.

### Social media observations (client to confirm current handles/metrics)
- No prominent, consistently posted Instagram/Facebook presence surfaced in research —
  discovery is driven mostly by **OTAs, the wedding directories, and the website**.
  That is the single biggest gap and the biggest opportunity.
- Strong **UGC potential**: weddings, fireworks, pool, and sunset photos guests already
  shoot are not being systematically captured, reposted, or turned into reels.
- Reviews exist across Tripadvisor/Yelp/Google but are likely **not actively solicited
  or routed** — a review engine can lift both rating and ranking.
- **Recommendation:** stand up a single, daily-posted "Coconut Cove" master brand on IG
  + FB with a link-in-bio hub that splits cleanly into **Book a stay / Plan a wedding /
  Marina & watersports.**

### Customer segments
1. **Couples & wedding parties** (highest value) — destination weddings, elopements,
   rehearsal dinners, vow renewals; long lead time, high ticket, research-heavy.
2. **Vacationers / families** (volume) — boutique Keys getaway, pool-and-beach families,
   couples on a quieter alternative to the big resorts.
3. **Boaters & anglers** — slip guests, watersports, dive/fishing charter day-trippers.
4. **Production / events** — film, commercial, and photo shoots; private buyouts.

### Competitor analysis (Islamorada lodging / wedding venues)

| Competitor | Strength | Gap a.i. STaRR exploits for Coconut Cove |
|---|---|---|
| **Cheeca Lodge & Spa** | Flagship brand, big budget, polished | Coconut Cove wins on intimacy, value, and a true *private-estate* wedding (whole place is yours) |
| **Postcard Inn / Holiday Isle** | Big beach-party energy, strong social | Coconut Cove is the quiet, romantic, all-in-one alternative — and owns the fireworks-wedding angle |
| **Amara Cay / Pelican Cove** | Modern boutique rooms | Coconut Cove pairs lodging *with* a marina + full wedding venue under one owner |
| **Generic Keys wedding venues** | One-off ceremony sites | Coconut Cove offers ceremony + reception + lodging + marina in one place — guests never leave |

**Insight:** Competitors win on a single hook (big-brand luxury, or beach party, or a
ceremony lawn). Coconut Cove's unfair advantage is the **bundle on one private
estate** — stay, wed, and play the water without ever leaving. That becomes the brand
narrative.

## 3. Marketing Strategy

**Positioning:** *"Your own piece of the Keys."* Coconut Cove is the intimate,
owner-run Islamorada estate where you can stay, get married, and live the water — all in
one place, all weekend long.

**Audiences & messages:**
- Couples/weddings → "Marry on a private Keys estate — sunset vows, fireworks, all night long."
- Vacationers/families → "The quiet Keys getaway: waterfall pool, private beach, your own cottage."
- Boaters/anglers → "Dock, dive, and explore — your marina home base in Islamorada."
- Production → "A turnkey Keys location for film, commercial, and photo shoots."

**Channels:** Instagram (primary), Facebook (events + weddings + locals), Google
Business + OTA review parity (discovery), Pinterest (weddings — high-intent), TikTok
(optional reel syndication), email/SMS (wedding-lead nurture + repeat guests).

**90-day arc:**
- **Days 1–30:** Stand up the unified brand, launch daily posting, deploy review engine
  + wedding-lead funnel. (This proposal's 30-day system.)
- **Days 31–60:** Layer premium video (hero film + wedding film), Pinterest wedding
  push, marina/watersports booking funnel.
- **Days 61–90:** Optimize from dashboard data, scale top content, add paid amplification
  on proven winners (wedding-inquiry and direct-booking campaigns).

### Dedicated TOURISM / Getaway marketing ideas
- **"Stay where the locals would" series** — owner-run, old-Keys authenticity vs. the
  big chains; lean into the 1994 heritage.
- **Walkable Islamorada guide** — Theater of the Sea, Windley Key Geological Park,
  restaurant strip — position Coconut Cove as the base camp you never need a car from.
- **Cottage-life content** — Captain's cottage with full kitchen + picnic table out
  front; "cook your catch, eat by the water."
- **Off-season "Keys for two" packages** — fill shoulder-season midweek rooms with a
  romance bundle (room + sunset dinner at the Tiki bar + late checkout).
- **Direct-booking push** — "Book direct, skip the OTA fee" link-in-bio hub to claw
  margin back from Expedia/Booking.

### Dedicated WEDDING marketing ideas
- **"Whole-estate wedding" hero angle** — the entire 7 acres is yours; guests stay
  on-site; the party goes all night under the covered venue.
- **Fireworks signature** — own the evening-fireworks finale as a unique, ownable,
  highly shareable visual; build the brand around it.
- **Real-wedding features** — recurring couple spotlights (photo carousels + reels) that
  double as social proof and SEO/Pinterest fuel.
- **Pinterest + WeddingWire/The Knot funnel** — high-intent boards (Keys beach wedding,
  Tiki reception, sunset vows) feeding the inquiry form.
- **Vendor co-marketing** — tag and cross-promote planners, florists, and photographers
  for reach and referrals.
- **Elopement / micro-wedding package** — capture the growing small-wedding market with
  a fast, fixed-price weekday offer.
- **Production crossover** — market the same estate to film/commercial/photo shoots in
  the off-season (a differentiated, high-margin lead line).

### Dedicated VACATION / getaway marketing ideas (water + amenities)
- **Marina home-base content** — slip life, "wake up on the water," boats to 30 ft.
- **Watersports day** — waverunners, kayaks, dive and fishing charters; "book the day"
  CTAs to partner operators.
- **Waterfall-pool hero content** — the zero-entry waterfall pool is a scroll-stopper;
  make it a recurring signature shot.
- **Family "unplug" angle** — quiet, gated, private-beach Keys time vs. crowded chains.

## 4. Social Media Strategy

**Content pillars (the "5 C's"):**
1. **Cove Life & Vibe (CV)** — waterfall pool, private beach, Tiki bar, golden hour, the
   old-Keys estate feel.
2. **Couples & Weddings (CW)** — sunset ceremonies, fireworks, real weddings, the venue,
   inquiry CTAs.
3. **Cottages & Stays (CS)** — Captain's cottages, General's rooms, packages, direct
   booking, the guest experience.
4. **Coast & Marina (CM)** — slips, watersports, dive/fishing charters, "wake up on the
   water," boating life.
5. **Community & Stories (CO)** — owner/heritage story, staff, guests, Islamorada
   neighbors, UGC reposts.

**Cadence (Growth tier):** 1 feed post/day + 1–2 stories/day; 8–10 reels/month; geotag
every post (Islamorada, Coconut Cove Resort & Marina); branded hashtag **#CoconutCoveKeys**
plus rotating local tags (#Islamorada #FloridaKeys #KeysWedding #VisitFloridaKeys).

**Engagement:** Reshare UGC daily, reply to comments/DMs within hours (AI-assisted
templates), monthly "tag your Coconut Cove moment" UGC prompt, and a fast-lane DM auto-
reply that routes wedding inquiries to the lead funnel.

## 10. AI Automation Opportunities

Using the standard a.i. STaRR stack (see [brand guide](../_shared/brand-guide.md#5-standard-ai-automation-stack)):

| Workflow | What it does | Tools | Hours saved/wk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Content engine** | Generates monthly calendar + draft posts from the 5 C pillars; routes to approval | ChatGPT/Claude + Google Sheets | ~5 |
| **Auto-posting** | Publishes approved posts/reels on schedule to IG + FB | Make.com + Meta API | ~4 |
| **Wedding-lead funnel** | Inquiry form + DM autoresponder → CRM → instant reply → 4-step nurture (brochure, dates, call, hold) | Make.com + Sheets + email/SMS | ~3 |
| **UGC capture** | Pulls tagged photos/mentions into a repost/review queue | Make.com + Meta API + Sheets | ~2 |
| **Review generation** | After checkout / event, triggers SMS/email review request to Google | Make.com + SMS/email | ~2 |
| **Reporting** | Pulls weekly metrics into a live dashboard | Make.com + Sheets/Looker Studio | ~1 |

**Wedding-lead funnel (the high-value one):** Single "Plan your Coconut Cove wedding"
form + IG/FB DM autoresponder → leads logged in Google Sheets CRM → instant auto-reply
with a brochure/date-check link → 4-step nurture sequence (info → date availability →
booking call → soft hold) → owner dashboard of open inquiries. One booked wedding pays
for many months of service.

**Review generation system:** Post-checkout or post-event trigger → timed SMS/email
asking for a Google review with a one-tap link → routes 5-star intent to public review,
flags <4-star privately to management for service recovery.

**Reporting dashboard:** One link showing followers, reach, top posts, reviews
generated, wedding leads captured, and direct bookings influenced — refreshed
automatically.

**Net effect:** ~15+ hours/week of manual marketing work removed while *increasing*
output to daily — without adding staff to an owner-run resort.

Per the [pricing framework](../_shared/pricing-framework.md), Coconut Cove's boutique
scale plus a high-value wedding funnel fits the **Growth** package, with a clear upgrade
path to Premium for peak wedding season.

| Option | Monthly | Fit |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | $1,500/mo | Single-focus test (lodging only, 1 platform) — not recommended given the wedding upside |
| **Growth (recommended)** ⭐ | **$3,000/mo** | 30 posts/mo, 8–10 reels, IG + FB, AI auto-posting, wedding-lead capture, live dashboard, 1 premium video/qtr |
| Premium | $5,500/mo | Peak-season scale: daily posting, 12+ reels, 3 platforms (add Pinterest/TikTok), full automation, 1 premium video/mo, monthly strategy call |
| Growth + Paid | $3,000/mo + 15% ad spend | Add wedding-inquiry + direct-booking ad campaigns once organic winners are proven |

**One-time build (credited into first 3 months):** 30-day content system $2,500 +
automation buildout (wedding funnel + posting + reviews + dashboard) $3,500 + photo/
video day to build the asset library $1,200 + optional hero brand video $1,500–$3,500.

**ROI framing:** A single booked **destination wedding** is typically worth many
thousands of dollars in venue + lodging revenue — meaning **one extra wedding inquiry
that converts can cover months of the Growth fee.** On lodging alone, at a ~$200+
average nightly rate, Growth pays for itself with roughly **15 extra room-nights per
month** — a low bar once daily posting + direct-booking links are live. (Rates/figures
*client to confirm.*)
