TREASURE HARBOR RESORT — 30-SECOND COMMERCIAL SCRIPT
=====================================================
Islamorada, Florida Keys
Voice Direction: Slow, cinematic, warm and deeply emotional. Breathe between lines.
Estimated Runtime: ~30 seconds at dramatic pace (~70 words)

------------------------------------------------------

[PAUSE — let silence breathe]

Close your eyes.

Feel the warm salt air on your face.
Hear the gentle lap of crystal water... beneath you.

Welcome to Treasure Harbor Resort.
Islamorada... the Heart of the Florida Keys.

Your houseboat. Your private harbor.
A front-row seat... to paradise.

Coral reefs at your doorstep.
Dolphins in the distance.
Sunsets... that steal your breath.

Snorkel. Fish. Drift. Dream.

This is your escape.

This... is Treasure Harbor.

[END]

- SFX: subtle waves, seagulls, gentle water lapping
- Pacing: deliberate pauses between lines for emotional weight
- Tone: wonder, serenity, longing, freedom
