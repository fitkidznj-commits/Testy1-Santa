## Service Overview

The **AI Intern** is a managed, part-time support service that puts an AI-driven team
member to work in your business **two days a week**. It handles the recurring,
time-eating tasks that keep small businesses stuck — content, research, CRM,
follow-up, notes, proposals, and data — so the owner gets hours back.

It runs on the standard
[a.i. STaRR AI stack](../_shared/brand-guide.md#5-standard-ai-automation-stack)
(Make.com, ChatGPT/Claude, Google Sheets, Canva, HeyGen/Creatomate, Meta + email/SMS),
with a human at a.i. STaRR reviewing every output before it reaches you. You get the
speed and tirelessness of AI with the judgment and polish of an agency.

**What makes it a force multiplier, not a tool:**

- It's *managed* — you don't prompt it, babysit it, or fix its mistakes.
- It's *on-brand* — output follows your voice and a.i. STaRR's quality bar.
- It's *finished* — work lands ready to send, post, or file, not as a rough draft.
- It *compounds* — every week the CRM gets cleaner, the content bank deeper, the
  follow-up tighter.

## Scope of Work

Two days a week across eight responsibilities. Every task uses the a.i. STaRR stack
and ships through human review.

### 1. Social content

| | |
|---|---|
| **What the AI Intern does** | Drafts a weekly batch of on-brand posts + captions from your content pillars, builds matching Canva graphics from templates, suggests hashtags/geotags, and queues for your approval |
| **Tools** | ChatGPT/Claude, Canva, Google Sheets (calendar), Make.com (scheduling) |
| **What the client gets** | A ready-to-approve post batch each week (copy + graphic + hashtags), scheduled on request |
| **Turnaround** | Weekly batch delivered on Day 1; revisions within 24 hrs |

### 2. Research

| | |
|---|---|
| **What the AI Intern does** | Runs competitor, market, pricing, prospect, or topic research and condenses it into a one-page brief with sources and a recommendation |
| **Tools** | ChatGPT/Claude + web research, Google Sheets/Docs |
| **What the client gets** | A cited research brief — findings, "so what," and a recommended next step |
| **Turnaround** | 24–48 hrs per brief |

### 3. CRM updates

| | |
|---|---|
| **What the AI Intern does** | Logs new contacts, dedupes and cleans records, updates stages/statuses, tags by segment, and flags stale or at-risk deals |
| **Tools** | Google Sheets CRM (or your CRM), Make.com |
| **What the client gets** | A clean, current CRM and a short "what changed / who to chase" summary |
| **Turnaround** | Updated each working day; weekly clean-up pass |

### 4. Lead generation

| | |
|---|---|
| **What the AI Intern does** | Builds targeted prospect lists, captures inbound web/DM inquiries into the CRM, and routes hot leads with suggested first-touch copy |
| **Tools** | ChatGPT/Claude, Google Sheets, Make.com, Meta API |
| **What the client gets** | A weekly list of qualified prospects + inbound leads logged and triaged |
| **Turnaround** | Inbound captured in near-real-time; prospect list weekly |

### 5. Follow-up emails

| | |
|---|---|
| **What the AI Intern does** | Drafts personalized follow-ups for leads, quotes, and past customers; builds simple sequences; flags who's gone quiet |
| **Tools** | ChatGPT/Claude, email/SMS, Make.com, Google Sheets |
| **What the client gets** | Ready-to-send follow-up drafts (or auto-sent on approval) and a "needs follow-up" list |
| **Turnaround** | Same-day for time-sensitive replies; sequences within 24 hrs |

### 6. Meeting notes

| | |
|---|---|
| **What the AI Intern does** | Turns a recording or rough notes into a clean summary with decisions, action items, owners, and due dates |
| **Tools** | ChatGPT/Claude (transcription + summarization), Google Docs |
| **What the client gets** | A tidy meeting summary + an action-item list routed to the CRM/task list |
| **Turnaround** | Same-day (within hours of receiving the recording/notes) |

### 7. Proposal drafting

| | |
|---|---|
| **What the AI Intern does** | Assembles first-draft proposals and quotes from your templates + the relevant CRM/research, formatted and on-brand |
| **Tools** | ChatGPT/Claude, Google Docs, [a.i. STaRR templates](../_shared/deliverable-template.md) |
| **What the client gets** | A polished draft proposal ready for your review and pricing sign-off |
| **Turnaround** | 24–48 hrs per proposal |

### 8. Data organization

| | |
|---|---|
| **What the AI Intern does** | Cleans and structures spreadsheets, files, and contacts; standardizes formats; builds simple trackers and a light weekly metrics snapshot |
| **Tools** | Google Sheets/Drive, Make.com, ChatGPT/Claude |
| **What the client gets** | Organized, consistent data + a one-glance snapshot of the week's numbers |
| **Turnaround** | Rolling; weekly tidy-up and snapshot |
