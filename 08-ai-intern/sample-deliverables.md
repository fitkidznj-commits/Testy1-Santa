## 2. Sample Research Brief

> **RESEARCH BRIEF — Islamorada wine-bar / small-plates competitive scan**
> Prepared by the AI Intern for Reef & Vine · 1 page

**Question:** Who are we competing with on "wine + small plates + sunset" in the
Upper Keys, and where's the open lane?

**Findings:**
- Most waterfront competitors lead on a single hook — sunset *or* seafood *or*
  nightlife — and treat wine as an afterthought (beer/cocktail-forward menus).
- Few local spots market a **curated wine program** + small plates as the main event.
- Instagram engagement in the area spikes on golden-hour and food-detail posts;
  team/heritage posts build loyalty but get posted rarely.

**So what:** Reef & Vine can own "the Keys' wine evening" — a lane no competitor is
clearly claiming. Pairings and the sommelier angle are differentiators worth posting
weekly.

**Recommended next step:** Make "wine pairing of the week" a standing content pillar
and a Thursday in-house special; test a sunset-flight promotion.

**Sources (illustrative):** competitor IG/Google Business profiles; local market
context per [Whale Harbor research](../01-whale-harbor/proposal.md#2-research-findings)
and the [Fiverr small-business marketing survey](https://block.fiverr.com/news/small-business-survey-2025).
*Specifics flagged "client to confirm" per the [house standard](../_shared/deliverable-template.md).*

## 3. Sample Follow-Up Email

> **To:** prospective private-event lead (booked a tasting inquiry, went quiet 4 days)
> **From:** Reef & Vine (drafted by the AI Intern, sent on owner approval)
> **Subject:** Your sunset tasting at Reef & Vine — still holding your date

Hi Marcus,

Thanks again for reaching out about a private wine tasting for your group of 12. I
wanted to follow up before the date fills — sunset Saturdays book out fast this time
of year.

Here's what we'd put together for you:

- A guided 5-pour flight, paired with a chef's small-plates board
- Your group's own corner of the patio for golden hour
- About 90 minutes, ~$55/guest (final menu your call)

Want me to pencil in **Saturday the 28th** while it's open? Just reply "hold it" and
it's yours — no commitment until you confirm the headcount.

Either way, glad you found us. The Keys do sunset right, and we'd love to pour for you.

Warmly,
The Reef & Vine team

*Follow-up logic: triggered when a lead goes quiet 3–4 days; tone matches
[brand voice](../_shared/brand-guide.md#3-voice--tone); routed back to CRM after send.*
