## Executive Summary

Most Keys business owners are great at the work and short on everything else. The
posts don't go out. The leads sit unanswered. The notes never get written up. The
CRM is a shoebox of business cards. Not because owners don't care — because there's
one of them, and the day runs out.

The **AI Intern** fixes that. For **$1,800/mo** you get a part-time, AI-driven
team member working **2 days a week** on the unglamorous, time-eating work that
keeps a small business stuck: social content, research, CRM updates, lead
generation, follow-up emails, meeting notes, proposal drafting, and data
organization.

This isn't a chatbot you have to manage. It's a managed service — a.i. STaRR runs the
AI systems and a human checks the output — so what lands on your desk is finished,
on-brand, and ready to use.

**The positioning in one line:** the AI Intern is a *force multiplier*. It doesn't
replace you; it gives you back the hours you're losing to busywork and turns one
owner into the output of a small team. Industry data says small business owners
spend **68% of their time on daily tasks** and lose roughly **96 minutes a day** to
busywork, while **70% spend under five hours a week on marketing**
([sources](service-overview.md#research-the-problem-were-solving)). The AI Intern is
built to claw those hours back.
