Companion to [`proposal.md`](proposal.md). Graphics/video delivered as concepts +
storyboards; a.i. STaRR renders finished cuts via HeyGen/Creatomate as an add-on.

## Video Strategy

- **Short-form reels carry the daily vibe** — sunsets, catches, feast, sandbar. High
  volume, trend-driven, shot on phone + drone.
- **5 premium videos anchor the brand** — evergreen hero films used on the website,
  ads, and pinned social.
- **AI acceleration:** a templated reel system in Creatomate turns a shared shot
  library + captions into branded reels on autopilot; HeyGen produces
  presenter/voiceover segments for promos and event recaps without a shoot.

## 5 Premium Video Concepts

### 1. "One Harbor. Your Whole Keys Day." — Hero Brand Film (90s)
The flagship. Follows a couple/family through a full day: sunrise charter, big catch,
sandbar swim, rooftop sunset cocktails, seafood feast under string lights. Cinematic
drone + handheld. Voiceover ties it together. Used on homepage, ads, pinned post.

### 2. Charter Film — "Reel In Your Story" (60s)
Fishing-forward: gear, mates, the run out, the fight, the photo, the dock. Sells the
charter experience to anglers and bucket-listers. Ends on the dock-to-dinner handoff.

### 3. Weddings & Events Film — "Say 'I Do' on the Water" (60s)
Romantic, golden-toned: ceremony with Atlantic backdrop, reception on the roof,
sparklers at sunset. Targets the wedding/events revenue line. DM/inquiry CTA.

### 4. Flagler Station Steakhouse Film — "Steak Meets Sea" (45s)
Upscale, moody, candlelit: the sear, the pour, the ocean view at dusk. Repositions
Flagler as the special-occasion dinner in the Upper Keys.

### 5. Seafood Feast Film — "Bring Your Appetite" (45s)
Abundance and energy: the buffet line, crab cracking, families digging in, the iconic
all-you-can-eat promise. Drives covers on feast nights.
