## 2. Research Findings

### The property (client to confirm specifics)
- **Location:** 83413 Overseas Hwy, Islamorada, FL — MM 83.5, ~2.6 oceanfront acres on the Atlantic.
- **Venues on site:**
  - **Wahoo's Bar & Grill** — multi-level restaurant, locally caught mahi, grouper, snapper, tuna, Florida lobster; "best unobstructed ocean views in the Keys."
  - **Whale Harbor Seafood Feast / Buffet** — large all-you-can-eat seafood buffet, a long-running Islamorada draw.
  - **Flagler Station Steakhouse** — newer upscale steakhouse, open Wed–Sun 5–10pm.
  - **SandBAR (rooftop)** — premier Islamorada sunset-cocktail spot overlooking the marina.
  - **Whale Harbor Sandbar (offshore)** — famous boating/party sandbar just off the marina.
  - **Marina + charters** — deep-sea fishing, party/head boats, sandbar shuttles.
- **Charter market context:** Area charters run ~$125/person up to $2,500+ for full-day
  swordfish trips; party/head boats ~$70/person; offshore split charters ~$475/person.

*Sources:* [Yelp – Whale Harbor Seafood Buffet](https://www.yelp.com/biz/whale-harbor-restaurant-and-seafood-buffet-islamorada-2),
[Wahoo's](https://www.wahoosbarandgrill.com/),

### Customer segments
1. **Tourists / vacationers** (primary) — looking for "the" Keys experience; high
   intent, short decision window, heavy on reviews and Instagram.
2. **Boaters & anglers** — charter bookings, sandbar day-trippers.
3. **Locals** — buffet regulars, sunset/happy-hour crowd, special occasions at Flagler.
4. **Events** — weddings, rehearsal dinners, corporate, sunset parties.

### Competitor analysis (Islamorada waterfront dining/marinas)

| Competitor | Strength | Gap a.i. STaRR exploits for Whale Harbor |
|---|---|---|
| **Lorelei / Cabana Bar** | Iconic sunset brand, strong social | Whale Harbor has *more* under one roof (charter+buffet+rooftop+steak) — sell the all-day story |
| **Morada Bay / Pierre's** | Polished beach-chic brand | Whale Harbor wins on authenticity + value + fishing heritage |
| **Bud n' Mary's Marina** | Fishing authority, charters | Whale Harbor pairs fishing with full dining + nightlife |
| **Islamorada Fish Company** | Brand recognition, retail | Whale Harbor's rooftop + offshore sandbar are unique |

**Insight:** Competitors win on a single hook (sunset, or fishing, or beach). Whale
Harbor's unfair advantage is the *combination* — no one else markets fish-to-feast-to-
sunset as one place. That becomes the brand narrative.

### Social media & website observations (client to confirm current handles/metrics)
- Multiple venue brands (Wahoo's, Whale Harbor Restaurant, SandBAR, Flagler) appear to
  run **separate or loosely connected** web/social presences → diluted audience,
  inconsistent posting, no unified story.
- Strong **user-generated content** exists (sunsets, big catches, sandbar days) but
  isn't being systematically captured, reposted, or turned into reels.
- Review volume is high but likely **not actively solicited or routed** — a review
  engine can lift both rating and ranking.
- **Recommendation:** a unified "Whale Harbor" master brand on social with venue
  sub-content, consistent daily posting, and a single link-in-bio booking hub.

## 3. Marketing Strategy

**Positioning:** *"One island. One harbor. Your whole Keys day."* Whale Harbor is the
all-day Islamorada destination — reel 'em in, soak up the sandbar, toast the sunset,
feast on the catch.

**Audiences & messages:**
- Tourists → "The only place you need to plan your Keys day around."
- Boaters/anglers → "Fish all morning, party the sandbar all afternoon, dock for dinner."
- Locals → "Your sunset spot, your seafood feast, your celebration table."
- Events → "Get married where the ocean meets the sunset."

**Channels:** Instagram (primary), Facebook (events + locals), Google Business
(reviews + discovery), TikTok (optional reels syndication), email/SMS (repeat + events).

**90-day arc:**
- **Days 1–30:** Build the unified brand presence, launch daily posting, deploy review
  engine. (This proposal's 30-day system.)
- **Days 31–60:** Layer premium video, events/weddings push, charter booking funnel.
- **Days 61–90:** Optimize from dashboard data, scale top content, introduce paid
  amplification on proven winners.

## 4. Social Media Strategy

**Content pillars (the "5 S's"):**
1. **Sunsets & Vibe** — rooftop SandBAR golden hour, atmosphere.
2. **Seafood & Feast** — buffet spreads, Wahoo's plates, Flagler steaks, fresh catch.
3. **Sandbar & Sea** — offshore sandbar days, charters, big catches, boating life.
4. **Stories & People** — captains, chefs, staff, regulars, Keys heritage.
5. **Specials & Events** — happy hour, live music, weddings, holidays, bookings.

**Cadence (Premium tier):** 1 feed post/day + 1–2 stories/day per platform; 8–12
reels/month; geotag every post (Islamorada, Whale Harbor Marina); branded hashtag
(#WhaleHarborDay) plus rotating local tags.

**Engagement:** Reshare UGC daily, reply to comments/DMs within hours (AI-assisted
templates), run monthly "tag your Whale Harbor day" UGC prompt.

## 10. AI Automation Opportunities

Using the standard a.i. STaRR stack (see [brand guide](../_shared/brand-guide.md#5-standard-ai-automation-stack)):

| Workflow | What it does | Tools | Hours saved/wk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Content engine** | Generates monthly calendar + draft posts from pillars; routes to approval | ChatGPT/Claude + Google Sheets | ~5 |
| **Auto-posting** | Publishes approved posts/reels on schedule to IG + FB | Make.com + Meta API | ~4 |
| **UGC capture** | Pulls tagged photos/mentions into a review/repost queue | Make.com + Meta API + Sheets | ~2 |
| **Review generation** | After charter/dinner, triggers SMS/email review request to Google | Make.com + SMS/email | ~2 |
| **Lead capture** | Web/DM inquiries (weddings, charters, parties) → CRM + auto-reply + follow-up sequence | Make.com + Sheets + email/SMS | ~3 |
| **Reporting** | Pulls weekly metrics into a live dashboard | Make.com + Sheets/Looker Studio | ~1 |

**Review generation system:** Post-experience trigger (POS/booking or manual tag) →
timed SMS/email asking for a Google review with a one-tap link → routes 5-star intent
to public review, flags <4-star privately to management for service recovery.

**Lead capture system:** Single "Plan your Whale Harbor day / book an event" form +
DM autoresponder → leads logged in Google Sheets CRM → instant auto-reply → 3-step
follow-up sequence → owner dashboard of open inquiries.

**Reporting dashboard:** One link showing followers, reach, top posts, reviews
generated, leads captured, and bookings influenced — refreshed automatically.

**Net effect:** ~15+ hours/week of manual marketing work removed while *increasing*
output to daily.
