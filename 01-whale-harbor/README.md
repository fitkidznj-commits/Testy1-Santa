# Project 1 — Whale Harbor Digital Marketing Proposal + 30-Day Content System

**Client:** Whale Harbor Marina & Restaurants — Islamorada, FL (MM 83.5, 83413 Overseas Hwy)
**Prepared by:** a.i. STaRR
**Goal:** A proposal that makes ownership feel a.i. STaRR already understands their
business better than most agencies — paired with a ready-to-run 30-day content system.

---

## Executive Summary

Whale Harbor is a Florida Keys institution: 2.6 oceanfront acres housing **Wahoo's Bar
& Grill**, the **Whale Harbor Seafood Feast/Buffet**, **Flagler Station Steakhouse**,
the rooftop **SandBAR**, a working **marina**, and the legendary offshore **Whale
Harbor Sandbar** — plus fishing charters and party boats. Few destinations in
Islamorada can sell the *entire* day: fish in the morning, sandbar at noon, sunset
cocktails on the roof, seafood feast at night.

The opportunity: that all-day story is rarely told as one coordinated narrative across
social. a.i. STaRR proposes a unified content system that markets Whale Harbor as
*the* Islamorada day-to-night destination, with daily posting, reels that capture the
sunset-and-seafood vibe, a review engine, and AI automation that keeps it all running.

**Headline outcome:** A complete 30-day content system live in 30 days, daily posting
across Instagram/Facebook, a review-generation engine, and a live performance
dashboard — positioning Whale Harbor as the most-followed waterfront destination in
the Upper Keys.

**Recommended package:** Premium ($5,500/mo) — multi-venue scope warrants daily
posting and full automation. See [`proposal.md`](proposal.md#11-pricing-recommendations).
