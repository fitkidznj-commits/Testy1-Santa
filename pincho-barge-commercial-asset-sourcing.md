### Foods mentioned (handle with care)

Reviewer comments consistently mention a **steak sandwich with fries** and **hot dogs**,
described as fresh and good. Those are the safest food references.

> **🚩 Fabrication risk flag:** Several search summaries pull menu items — a *"Tostón
> Burger,"* grilled *"Pincho"* skewers, and a loaded bacon-pineapple hot dog — from
> **`pincho.com`, which is a separate Miami burger-restaurant chain called PINCHO.**
> It is **not confirmed** that The Pincho Barge shares that menu or is affiliated with
> that chain. **Do not put those specific items in the commercial** unless a human
> confirms them directly on the @pinchobarge pages. The request explicitly says: *do
> not invent menu items.* This is the single biggest trap in this project.

**Do not invent, and confirm on official pages before using:** exact menu, prices,
operating hours/schedule, phone number, and any claim of affiliation with the PINCHO
restaurant chain.
