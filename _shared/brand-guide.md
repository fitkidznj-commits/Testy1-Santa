## 1. Who We Are

**a.i. STaRR** is an AI-powered marketing and business-growth company built for the
Florida Keys. We combine agency-level creative (brand, content, video, social) with
AI automation (Make.com, ChatGPT, Creatomate, HeyGen) so local businesses get the
output of a full marketing team at a fraction of the cost and time.

**Tagline options:**
- *"Marketing that runs itself."*
- *"Local soul. AI speed."*
- *"Your marketing team — powered by AI, rooted in the Keys."*

**One-line positioning:**
We help Florida Keys businesses get found, get followed, and get booked — by pairing
human creative direction with AI systems that produce and publish content every day.

## 3. Voice & Tone

- **Confident, not corporate.** Speak like a trusted local partner who happens to be
  brilliant at marketing.
- **Warm + Keys-authentic.** Saltwater, sunsets, community, tradition — never
  cheesy-touristy.
- **Clear over clever.** Short sentences. Plain English. Specific numbers.
- **Proof-forward.** Show before/after, examples, and concrete deliverables.

**Words we like:** local, fresh, daily, booked, effortless, system, story, community.
**Words we avoid:** synergy, leverage (in client copy), disrupt, ninja, guru.
