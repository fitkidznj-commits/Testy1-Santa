# a.i. STaRR — Pricing Framework

> Reusable pricing tiers referenced by every project proposal. Figures are
> recommended list pricing for the Florida Keys market and should be tailored to each
> client's scope. All prices USD.

---

## 1. Core Monthly Packages

| Tier | Best for | Monthly | Includes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | Single location, getting consistent | **$1,500/mo** | 12 posts/mo, 4 reels, 1 platform managed, monthly report, review requests |
| **Growth** ⭐ | Most local businesses | **$3,000/mo** | 30 posts/mo, 8–10 reels, 2 platforms, AI auto-posting, lead capture, live dashboard, 1 premium video/qtr |
| **Premium** | Multi-venue / aggressive growth | **$5,500/mo** | Daily posting, 12+ reels, 3 platforms, full automation suite, 1 premium video/mo, review engine, monthly strategy call |

*3-month minimum recommended. Month-to-month after.*

## 4. Pricing Principles

- **Lead with value, anchor with the Growth tier.** Most clients land on Growth.
- **Bundle the build into the retainer** when it wins the deal (waive/credit setup).
- **Always show ROI math:** cost of one new booking/customer vs. monthly fee.
- **Local discount lever:** offer founding-client or referral pricing, not random
  discounts.
- Figures are starting points — confirm scope before quoting a specific client.
