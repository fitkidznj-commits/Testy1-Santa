# a.i. STaRR — Standard 13-Section Deliverable Template

> Every project in this repository follows this section order so each proposal feels
> uniform, complete, and presentation-ready. Adapt depth to the project, but keep the
> headings and sequence.

---

1. **Executive Summary** — Who the client is, the opportunity, what a.i. STaRR
   proposes, and the headline outcome. One scannable page.

2. **Research Findings** — Business research, competitor analysis, and local-market
   insight. Cite sources. Flag unverified items as *"client to confirm."*

3. **Marketing Strategy** — Positioning, target audiences, key messages, channels,
   and the 90-day arc.

4. **Social Media Strategy** — Platform plan, content pillars, posting cadence,
   hashtag/geo strategy, engagement plan.

5. **30-Day Content Plan** — A day-by-day calendar table (date, platform, pillar,
   format, hook, CTA), plus the completed sample posts/captions.

6. **Video Strategy** — Reel approach, premium video concepts, and how AI video
   (HeyGen/Creatomate) accelerates production.

7. **Sample Posts** — Ready-to-publish post copy with captions and CTAs.

8. **Sample Reels** — Reel concepts with hook, shot list, on-screen text, audio, CTA.

9. **Sample Graphics** — Graphic concepts as art-direction specs (layout, copy,
   color, imagery) ready to build in Canva.

10. **AI Automation Opportunities** — Specific Make.com workflows, the stack used,
    and the hours saved. Reference `_shared/brand-guide.md` stack.

11. **Pricing Recommendations** — Recommended tier/package from
    `_shared/pricing-framework.md`, tailored to this client, with ROI framing.

12. **Proposal Presentation Outline** — Slide-by-slide outline for the pitch meeting.

13. **Next-Step Action Plan** — Concrete next steps, timeline, and what a.i. STaRR
    needs from the client to start.

---

### Conventions
- Use tables for calendars, pricing, and competitor comparisons.
- Graphics and video are delivered as **concepts/specs/storyboards** in markdown;
  note Canva/HeyGen rendering as an available production upsell.
- Keep claims grounded; label assumptions *"client to confirm."*
